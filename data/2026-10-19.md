### [1] [On the complexification of $ q $-deformations of compact semisimple Lie groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23236)
*Heon Lee,Christian Voigt*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文研究紧致半单量子群上正则函数代数的本原谱与有界算子不可约表示的关系，证明了同质层与异质层本原理想的尖锐二分现象，并刻画了Drinfeld double的Iwasawa分解与量子全纯赋值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子群上本原谱（纯粹代数对象）与希尔伯特空间上有界算子的拓扑不可约表示（解析对象）之间是否存在一一对应，是算子代数量子群理论的中心问题；经典情形下两者通过全纯赋值相联系，量子情形尚不清楚。

Method: 借助Joseph分层理论对本原谱进行分层；通过Drinfeld double构造量子全纯赋值；并用多项式函数描述Drinfeld double的Iwasawa分解，将Podleś–Woronowicz关于量子Lorentz群的结果推广到所有复半单量子群。

Result: 证明了尖锐二分：同质层中的本原理想总能实现为希尔伯特空间上有界算子拓扑不可约表示的核，而异质层中的本原理想不存在此类实现；与经典情形不同，量子全纯赋值通常不是拓扑不可约的，且其核不能决定本原谱。

Conclusion: 量子情形下本原谱与拓扑不可约表示之间存在本质性的结构差异，量子全纯赋值不足以刻画本原谱；Iwasawa分解为理解量子全纯赋值的结构提供了统一框架。

Abstract: For the quantised algebra of functions on a simply connected compact semisimple Lie group, we study the relationship between the primitive spectrum, topologically irreducible representations by bounded operators on Hilbert spaces, and quantum holomorphic evaluations induced via the Drinfeld double. In terms of the Joseph stratification of the primitive spectrum, we prove a sharp dichotomy: primitive ideals in homogeneous strata always arise as kernels of topologically irreducible representations by bounded operators on a Hilbert space, whereas those in inhomogeneous strata do not admit any such realisations. We further show that, unlike in the classical situation, quantum holomorphic evaluations typically fail to be topologically irreducible, and that their kernels do not determine the primitive spectrum. Finally, we describe the Iwasawa decomposition of the Drinfeld double in terms of polynomial functions, extending work by Podleś-Woronowicz for the quantum Lorentz group to all complex semisimple quantum groups, and clarifying the structure of quantum holomorphic evaluations.

### [2] [Quantum Periods from TBA and Continued Fractions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22571)
*Alexander Chervov,Ilia Gaiur,Sergey Galkin,Fedor Levkovich-Maslyuk,Volodya Rubtsov,Shamil Shakirov*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文研究 local P¹×P¹ 的量子 A-周期，即 q-交换变量下 Laurent 多项式 x+x⁻¹+y+y⁻¹ 幂的常数项生成函数，给出 TBA 方程刻画高阶对角模、连分数与正交多项式新公式、显式递推，并指出经典周期的 holonomic 性不能推广为量子情形的 q-holonomic 性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子 A-周期是镜像对称与枚举几何中的核心对象，但其组合与代数结构尚缺乏系统刻画；作者希望为 local P¹×P¹ 这一典型例子建立多种新表示，并探究经典周期方程的可积性（holonomicity）在量子形变下是否保持。

Method: 以 q-交换变量的非交换 Laurent 多项式为出发点，用 TBA（Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz）方程推导 A-周期及高阶对角模；借助连分数与相关正交多项式给出周期的新表达式；构造非平凡的显式递推以实现高效计算；并对经典 Picard-Fuchs 方程计算量子修正，检验其 q-holonomic 性质。

Result: 发现 TBA 方程不仅编码 A-周期本身，还编码对应非交换 Laurent 多项式的高阶对角模；得到周期的连分数与正交多项式新公式及高效显式递推；算出了经典 Picard-Fuchs 方程的量子修正，并观察到经典周期的 holonomicity 并不推广为量子周期的 q-holonomicity。

Conclusion: 该工作为 local P¹×P¹ 的量子 A-周期提供了多种新表示与计算工具，揭示了量子情形下可积性结构的本质变化，并指出其在 Heisenberg 群 Cayley 图上随机游走及更高效生成式 AI 系统方面的潜在应用。

Abstract: We consider quantum A-periods of local ${\mathbb P}^1 \times {\mathbb P}^1$, or equivalently the generating function of constant terms for powers of the Laurent polynomial $x + x^{-1} + y + y^{-1}$ where $x$ and $y$ are $q$-commuting variables. We derive a number of new representations for these rich combinatorial and algebraic objects. First, we find that the well-known TBA equation encodes not only the A-period itself but also higher diagonal modes of the corresponding noncommutative Laurent polynomials. Second, we obtain new formulas for the period in terms of continued fractions and related orthogonal polynomials, as well as a nontrivial explicit recursion allowing its efficient computation. Finally, we compute quantum corrections to the classical Picard-Fuchs equation, and observe that holonomicity of the classical period does not generalise to $q$-holonomicity of its quantum counterpart. Applications to random walks on the Cayley graph of the Heisenberg group, and related new approaches to more efficient generative AI systems, are outlined.

### [4] [Typical representations of admissible type I algebraic supergroups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23055)
*Taiki Shibata*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 研究了可容许 type I 代数超群 $G$ 的典型表示，证明 $H^0_G(\lambda)$ 单模性可由 $\lambda$ 的典型性与偶子群 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ 上 $H^0_{G_{\mathrm{ev}}}(\lambda)$ 的单模性共同刻画，并建立典型 block 与 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ 的 block 之间的等价关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超群表示论中 Kac 模与诱导模的不可约性、block 结构是核心问题。经典（非超）情形已有成熟理论，而超情形下"典型性"（typicality）与约化群情形的联系尚需澄清，尤其是诱导模 $H^0_G(\lambda)$ 何时为单模、典型 block 如何与偶子群 block 对应。

Method: 对可容许 type I 代数超群 $G$ 及其偶子群 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$，借助主导权 $\lambda$ 的典型性条件，比较 $G$ 与 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ 上的诱导超模 $H^0_G(\lambda)$ 与诱导模 $H^0_{G_{\mathrm{ev}}}(\lambda)$；建立典型 block 范畴与 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ 的 block 之间的等价；对 Frobenius 核上的 Kac 模给出投射性判别准则；再在奇特征代数闭域上对连通 $SpO(2\ell|2)$ 的 Frobenius 核分类单投射超模。

Result: 证明对主导权 $\lambda$，$H^0_G(\lambda)$ 为单模当且仅当 $\lambda$ 典型且 $H^0_{G_{\mathrm{ev}}}(\lambda)$ 为单模；建立 $G$ 的典型 block 与 $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ 的 block 之间的等价；得到每个 Frobenius 核上 Kac 模的投射性判别准则；并对奇特征域上连通的 $SpO(2\ell|2)$，完成其 Frobenius 核上所有单投射超模的分类。

Conclusion: 典型表示理论可将代数超群的单模性与 block 结构有效归约到偶子群情形，Kac 模的投射性可由统一准则判定；在 $SpO(2\ell|2)$ 这一具体超群上，Frobenius 核的单投射超模获得完整分类，为超群表示的分类研究提供了可推广的框架。

Abstract: We study typical representations of an admissible type I algebraic supergroup $G$. We prove that, for a dominant weight $λ$, the induced supermodule $H^0_G(λ)$ is simple if and only if $λ$ is typical and the induced module $H^0_{G_{\mathrm{ev}}}(λ)$ for the even subgroup $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$ is simple. We establish equivalences between typical blocks for $G$ and blocks for $G_{\mathrm{ev}}$, and give a projectivity criterion for Kac modules over every Frobenius kernel. For connected $SpO(2\ell|2)$ over an algebraically closed field of odd characteristic, we classify all simple projective supermodules over Frobenius kernels.

TL;DR: 该文证明跳跃环路给出的外同态 θ_k 的 Sutherland 障碍等于 k 倍正基本类，且 θ_k 忠实、构成 G-kernel；由此对任意单连通半单紧群 G，H^4(BG,Z) 中每个类都可由超有限 III_1 因子上的 G-kernel 实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究局部因子（超有限 III_1 因子）上群作用的外自同构与上同调障碍之间的关系，刻画跳跃环路构造给出的 G-kernel，并探讨 H^4(BG,Z) 中类的可实现性。

Method: 定义由跳跃环路诱导的外同态 θ_k 并计算其 Sutherland 障碍；在 A、D、E 型中将极大环面跳跃环路与伴随论文的格 α-归纳构造在流与荷电生成元上比较，再作整系数四次 Cartan 限制得 level-one 类；高 level 用张量可加性，其余型用指数一嵌入到单链群限制得到。

Conclusion: 对任意满足条件的 G，H^4(BG,Z) 的每一类都是超有限 III_1 因子上某个 G-kernel 的障碍，从而给出该类上同调群的完整实现。

Abstract: Let $G$ be a compact simply connected simple Lie group and $k \geq 1$. Jump loops give an outer homomorphism $θ_k$ from $G$ to the outer automorphisms of the local factor of the level-$k$ loop group net, which is the hyperfinite type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor. We show that its Sutherland obstruction is $k$ times the positive basic class in $H^3_{\mathrm{Borel}}(G, \mathbb{T}) \cong H^{4}(BG, \mathbb{Z})$ and that $θ_k$ is faithful, so it is a $G$-kernel. Central elements act by the outer automorphisms of the simple currents. In types A, D, E the level-one class is computed by comparing maximal-torus jump loops with the lattice $α$-induction construction of the companion paper on both current and charged generators, followed by integral degree-four Cartan restriction. Higher levels follow by tensor additivity, and the remaining types by restriction along index-one embeddings into simply laced groups. As a consequence, for every compact simply connected semisimple $G$, every class in $H^4(BG, \mathbb{Z})$ is the obstruction of a $G$-kernel on the hyperfinite type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 在词层面工作：把每个容许词写成Hoffman词与显式汇合关系的$\mathbb{Z}_{(2)}$-线性组合，其中$\mathbb{Z}_{(2)}$为2局部化整数环，保证系数为奇分母。再将此词层面结论传递给多重zeta值，并结合Brown定理与Furusho用汇合关系对Grothendieck–Teichmüller群$\mathrm{GRT}$的刻画进行推论。
