### [1] [The Haagerup VOA at $c=8$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13933)
*Andrew Riesen*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 构造中心荷8的强有理顶点算子代数 U，实现 Haagerup 双模范畴的 ribbon 等价。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在 V_{E_6⊕A_2} 内寻找实现 Haagerup 双的顶点算子代数结构。

Method: 由六个仿射流和三个指定权二场生成；结合格点计算、全权强生成归纳、C_2-余有限性、Zhu 代数角区的半单下降、模 Galois 对称及全纯 E_8 扩张。

Result: 得到中心荷8的单位强有理顶点算子代数 U，其权一李代数为 sl_2⊕sl_2，水平分别为1和39，并给出 Rep(U)≃Z(H_3) 的 ribbon 等价。

Conclusion: 该构造显式实现了 Haagerup 双，连接了顶点算子代数与子因子理论中的模范畴。

Abstract: We announce an explicit construction of a unitary, strongly rational vertex operator algebra $U$ of central charge $8$ inside $V_{E_6\oplus A_2}$, with a ribbon equivalence $\mathrm{Rep}(U)\simeq Z(\mathcal H_3)$ realizing the Haagerup double. The vertex algebra is generated by six affine currents and three specified weight-two fields; its weight-one Lie algebra is $\mathfrak{sl}_2\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2$, at levels $1$ and $39$. We outline the argument, which combines exact lattice calculations, an all-weight strong-generation induction and $C_2$-cofiniteness, semisimple descent for a full corner of Zhu's algebra, modular Galois symmetry, and holomorphic $E_8$ extensions.

</details>


### [2] [Haar measure for totally positive split real semisimple quantum groups of classical type](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13213)
*Kenny De Commer,Geoffrey Janssens,Wannes Malfait*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文构造了一个局部紧量子群，其整合了Drinfeld–Jimbo量子包络代数的Borel部分，并计算了Haar权与模数据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究如何从解析角度实现量子群的Borel部分，将其升级为局部紧量子群，并刻画其模结构，以连接经典的极大正半群函数代数。

Method: 设ħ为非零实数，q=e^{-πiħ}；对经典型分裂实半单Lie群G及其Borel子代数b，构造局部紧量子群(L_ħ(B),Δ)，使其整合U_q(b)作为Hopf *-代数，并计算Haar权与模数据。

Result: 证明了(L_ħ(B),Δ)的存在性，并显式计算了相应的Haar权与模数据；其Drinfeld double在相差多余Cartan部分的意义下，可视为G中全正半群G^+函数代数的解析量子化。

Conclusion: 该工作为量子群的解析实现提供了框架，并通过Drinfeld double将Borel量子群与全正半群函数代数的量子化联系起来。

Abstract: Let $\hbar$ be a non-zero real number and put $q= e^{-πi \hbar}$. Let $G$ be a semisimple split real Lie group of classical type, with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ and associated Borel subalgebra $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{g}$. We show that there exists a locally compact quantum group $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ which integrates the Borel part $U_q(\mathfrak{b})$ of the Drinfeld--Jimbo quantized enveloping algebra $U_q(\mathfrak{g})$, viewed as a Hopf $*$-algebra. We further compute the associated Haar weights and modular data for $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$. Up to an excess Cartan part, the Drinfeld double of $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ can be seen as an analytic quantization of the function algebra of the totally positive semigroup $G^+\subseteq G$.

</details>

*Luciana Basualdo Bonatto,Marcy Robertson*

Main category: math.AT

TL;DR: 本文构造了建模高亏格Teichmüller塔的映射类群胚的模operad S，给出其映射的呈现定理，并以此获得子群Γ̂对Ŝ的忠实作用及忠实Galois作用；亏格零截断等同于带括号ribbon辫的循环operad，其对象固定profinite自同构群为GT̂。完备化分类空间构成模∞-operad，其花冠值为带非零切向量的曲线模栈的几何étale同伦型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 构造映射类群胚的模operad S；用亏格零与亏格一生成元及其关系证明从S出发的映射的呈现定理；应用Nakamura–Schneps的工作得到Γ̂ ⊆ GT̂对Ŝ的忠实作用；考察亏格零截断与带括号ribbon辫循环operad的等同；取完备化分类空间得到模∞-operad。

The $\widehatΓ$-action induces a faithful action by homotopy automorphisms on the associated tower of profinite homotopy $1$-types, which agrees with the étale model in genus zero and one.

*Yingjin Bi*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文给出对称箭图Hecke代数上行列式模的Lusztig参数的显式公式，并在有限型A、D、E中借助Demazure织法识别Ménard种子中模的参数，进而分解前缀行列式模。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 行列式模及其Lusztig参数在对称箭图Hecke代数的表示论与簇代数结构中具有核心地位，但此前缺乏对任意Bruhat对及任意固定约化表达式的统一显式描述，且Ménard种子中模的参数识别与前缀行列式模的分解尚未明确。

Method: 作者首先推导出适用于每个Bruhat对及上Weyl群元素任意固定约化表达式的Lusztig参数显式公式；随后在有限型A、D、E中，利用Demazure织法（Demazure weaves）来识别Ménard种子中模的Lusztig参数；最后将前缀行列式模分解为这些种子模，其重数由Lusztig循环给出。

Result: 得到了行列式模Lusztig参数的通用显式公式；在A、D、E型中完成了Ménard种子模参数的识别；实现了前缀行列式模向种子模的分解，重数用Lusztig循环表达；并给出识别固定种子中单项式的判别准则及由Lusztig参数恢复其重数的显式公式。

Abstract: We give an explicit formula for the Lusztig parameters of determinantial modules over symmetric quiver Hecke algebras. The formula applies to every Bruhat pair and every fixed reduced expression of the upper Weyl group element. In finite types $A$, $D$, and $E$, we identify the parameters of the modules in Ménard's seed using Demazure weaves. We then factor prefix determinantial modules into these seed modules, with multiplicities given by Lusztig cycles. We also give a criterion for recognizing monomials in this fixed seed and an explicit formula recovering their multiplicities from Lusztig parameters.

### [6] [Right representations of Novikov algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16431)
*A. S. Panasenko*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究Novikov代数的右表示理论，引入模右理想、本原Novikov代数与准核概念，并证明无不可约表示构成遗传根，且该根等于所有不可约表示准核之交。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Novikov代数的表示理论尚不完善，缺乏类似结合代数的本原性与根理论框架。作者希望建立右表示的基本结构定理，并刻画无不可约表示的代数类，从而发展与Jacobson根相平行的理论。

Method: 引入模右理想概念，证明不可约右表示同构于极大模右理想的商；定义本原Novikov代数并刻画其与几乎忠实不可约表示的关系；引入表示的准核（kernel中最大的理想）。

Result: 证明每个不可约右表示同构于极大模右理想的商；无不可约表示的Novikov代数类构成遗传根；代数本原当且仅当存在几乎忠实的不可约表示；所构造的根恰等于所有不可约表示准核的交。

Conclusion: 建立了Novikov代数右表示的模右理想理论，给出本原代数与根的完整刻画，构造的根可视为Jacobson型根，为Novikov代数表示论提供了统一框架。

Abstract: We study the theory of right representations of Novikov algebras. We introduce the notion of a modular right ideal of a Novikov algebra and prove that every irreducible right representation of a Novikov algebra is isomorphic to the quotient by a maximal modular right ideal.
  We prove that the class of Novikov algebras with no irreducible representations forms a hereditary radical. We introduce the notion of a primitive Novikov algebra and show that a Novikov algebra is primitive if and only if it admits an almost faithful irreducible representation. We also introduce the notion of the quasi-kernel of a representation, defined as the largest ideal contained in its kernel. Finally, we prove that the constructed radical coincides with the intersection of the quasi-kernels of all its irreducible representations.

</details>
