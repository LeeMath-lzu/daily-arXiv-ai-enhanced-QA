### [3] [Lusztig Parameters and Factorizations of Determinantial Modules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12531)
*Yingjin Bi*

Main category: math.RT

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 行列式模是对称箭图Hecke代数表示论中的核心对象，其Lusztig参数刻画了模的精细结构。已有工作对Lusztig参数缺乏适用于任意Bruhat对和任意固定约化表达式的显式描述，且Ménard种子与实际行列式模参数之间的关系尚不清晰，需要建立统一的显式框架。

Method: 文章首先推导出对每个Bruhat对以及上Weyl群元素任意固定约化表达式均成立的Lusztig参数显式公式。随后在有限型A、D、E中，利用Demazure编织（weaves）来识别Ménard种子中模的Lusztig参数，并借此将前缀行列式模分解为这些种子模，分解的重数由Lusztig循环给出。此外，作者还建立了在该固定种子中识别单项式的判别准则，并给出从Lusztig参数恢复其重数的显式公式。

Result: 获得了适用于所有Bruhat对和任意固定约化表达式的Lusztig参数显式公式；在有限型A、D、E中用Demazure编织识别出Ménard种子模的参数；实现了前缀行列式模关于种子模的分解，重数由Lusztig循环表达；得到了种子中单项式的识别准则及重数恢复公式。

Conclusion: 该工作为对称箭图Hecke代数上行列式模提供了统一的显式参数理论，将Lusztig参数、Ménard种子与Demazure编织联系起来，并在有限型A、D、E中给出了种子分解与重数计算的完整工具。

Abstract: We give an explicit formula for the Lusztig parameters of determinantial modules over symmetric quiver Hecke algebras. The formula applies to every Bruhat pair and every fixed reduced expression of the upper Weyl group element. We then factor prefix determinantial modules into these seed modules, with multiplicities given by Lusztig cycles. We also give a criterion for recognizing monomials in this fixed seed and an explicit formula recovering their multiplicities from Lusztig parameters.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为Novikov代数的表示论建立类似结合代数中本原性与Jacobson根的理论框架。

Abstract: We study the theory of right representations of Novikov algebras. We introduce the notion of a modular right ideal of a Novikov algebra and prove that every irreducible right representation of a Novikov algebra is isomorphic to the quotient by a maximal modular right ideal.

We introduce the notion of a primitive Novikov algebra and show that a Novikov algebra is primitive if and only if it admits an almost faithful irreducible representation. We also introduce the notion of the quasi-kernel of a representation, defined as the largest ideal contained in its kernel. Finally, we prove that the constructed radical coincides with the intersection of the quasi-kernels of all its irreducible representations.

### [7] [Haar measure for totally positive split real semisimple quantum groups of classical type](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13213)
*Kenny De Commer,Geoffrey Janssens,Wannes Malfait*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文构造并研究了一个局部紧量子群，用以积分经典型分裂实半单Lie群的Borel部分的Drinfeld–Jimbo量子包络代数，并计算了Haar权重与模数据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将Drinfeld–Jimbo量子包络代数中的Borel部分$U_q(\mathfrak{b})$作为Hopf $*$-代数加以积分，构造对应的局部紧量子群，从而在解析层面理解量子群的Borel部分及其与全正半群的关系。

Method: 取非零实数$\hbar$，令$q=e^{-\pi i\hbar}$。对经典型分裂实半单Lie群$G$（Lie代数$\mathfrak{g}$、Borel子代数$\mathfrak{b}$），构造一个局部紧量子群$(L_{\hbar}(B),\Delta)$，使其积分$U_q(\mathfrak{b})$为Hopf $*$-代数，并计算相关Haar权重与模数据。

Result: 证明了局部紧量子群$(L_{\hbar}(B),\Delta)$的存在性，并计算了其Haar权重与模数据；此外，$(L_{\hbar}(B),\Delta)$的Drinfeld double在除去多余的Cartan部分后，可视为全正半群$G^+\subseteq G$函数代数的解析量子化。

Conclusion: 该工作为量子包络代数的Borel部分提供了局部紧量子群的解析实现，并将其Drinfeld double与全正半群的函数代数量子化联系起来。

Abstract: Let $\hbar$ be a non-zero real number and put $q= e^{-πi \hbar}$. Let $G$ be a semisimple split real Lie group of classical type, with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ and associated Borel subalgebra $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{g}$. We show that there exists a locally compact quantum group $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ which integrates the Borel part $U_q(\mathfrak{b})$ of the Drinfeld--Jimbo quantized enveloping algebra $U_q(\mathfrak{g})$, viewed as a Hopf $*$-algebra. We further compute the associated Haar weights and modular data for $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$. Up to an excess Cartan part, the Drinfeld double of $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ can be seen as an analytic quantization of the function algebra of the totally positive semigroup $G^+\subseteq G$.

</details>
