### [1] [The weak bialgebra structures on $\mathbb{k}^{\oplus n}$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26770)
*Jingheng Zhou*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文系统分类了有限维半单代数k^⊕n上的所有（弱）双代数结构，证明了双代数结构与有限幺半群一一对应、弱双代数结构与有限小范畴一一对应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 弱双代数是双代数的自然推广，在量子群、张量范畴和表示论中具有基础性作用，但其结构分类问题尚未被系统解决。

Method: 以具有本原正交幂等元判别基的有限维半单代数k^⊕n为研究对象，分别对双代数情形和弱双代数情形进行结构刻画与分类。

Result: 双代数结构与有限幺半群之间存在一一对应关系，且双代数同构恰好对应幺半群同构；弱双代数结构则唯一确定一个有限小范畴。

Abstract: Weak bialgebras are natural generalizations of bialgebras that play a fundamental role in quantum groups, tensor categories, and representation theory. In this paper, we systematically classify all (weak) bialgebra structures on the finite-dimensional semisimple algebra \(\Bbbk^{\oplus n}\), with a distinguished basis of primitive orthogonal idempotents. For the bialgebra case, we prove that such structures are in bijection with finite monoids, and that bialgebra isomorphisms correspond exactly to monoid isomorphisms. Extending the result to weak bialgebras, we show that every weak bialgebra structure uniquely determines a finite small category.

### [5] [Leading term strandings for webs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27425)
*Michael Bo,Madelyn Burns,Junyang Chen,Blaise Marsho,Jacob Martin,Jade Mawn,Bella Mohren,Heather M. Russell,Caitlin Sales,Lance Wong*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究sl_n webs的leading term strandings，证明其所有开放弦均为顺时针方向，并据此给出判定一组webs构成web basis的充分条件。作者从row-strict tableau构造具有指定leading term的web，得到Fontaine sl_n web bases的非递归构造。对于无平顶点的sl_3 webs，利用面深度识别leading term stranding；对于一般sl_3 webs，证明任意stranding可通过strand reversals操作序列达到leading term stranding。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: sl_n webs编码量子群张量积表示中的不变量向量，而stranding记录该向量的某个单项式。识别leading term stranding（即在词典序下编码leading term的stranding）对于理解web basis的结构至关重要，但此前缺乏系统的识别与构造方法。

Method: 首先证明leading term stranding的所有开放弦都是顺时针的，从而约束边界数据并获得web basis的充分判据。然后利用row-strict tableau构造具有预设leading term的web，提供Fontaine web bases的非递归构造。对于sl_3 webs，分别利用面深度识别无平顶点情形下的leading term stranding，并通过strand reversals操作从任意stranding到达leading term stranding。

Result: 给出了leading term stranding开放弦顺时针的定理；构造了非递归的Fontaine sl_n web bases；对sl_3无平顶点webs给出了基于面深度的leading term stranding识别方法；证明了任意sl_3 web的任意stranding可通过strand reversals序列到达leading term stranding。

Conclusion: 本文系统地刻画了sl_n webs的leading term strandings，提供了从构造到识别的完整框架，并建立了与Fontaine web bases之间的非递归联系，为web basis理论提供了新的工具和视角。

Abstract: A web is a plane graph encoding an invariant vector in a tensor product of fundamental representations of a quantum group. A stranding of an $\mathfrak{sl}_n$ web is a system of colored oriented curves recording one monomial of the vector it encodes. This article focuses on identifying and constructing leading term strandings, those recording the leading term of a web's vector with respect to a lexicographic order on monomials. We show that every open strand of a leading term stranding is clockwise, which constrains the boundary data of such strandings enough to yield a sufficient criterion for a set of webs to form a web basis. From a row-strict tableau, we construct a web with a prescribed leading term, and the resulting webs form a web basis, giving a non-recursive construction of Fontaine's $\mathfrak{sl}_n$ web bases. For $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ webs with no flat vertices, we identify a leading term stranding using the depths of the faces of the web. Finally, we show a leading term stranding for any $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ web can be reached from an arbitrary stranding via a sequence of operations called strand reversals.

### [6] [Quantum Cocycle Invariants of Knots from Yang-Baxter Cohomology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04267)
*Masahico Saito,Emanuele Zappala*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文从形变理论的视角，利用Yang-Baxter上同调的2-上循环构造了链环的量子上循环不变量，并证明它能够统一解释quandle上循环不变量，且在特定例子中比量子版本更强。作者还展示了该框架可导出Jones多项式和Alexander多项式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Yang-Baxter算子（YBO）和quandle上同调各自已发展出独立的链环不变量理论，且两者之间存在平行关系（2-上循环可解释为形变）。作者希望在此平行基础上，从形变理论的视角将Yang-Baxter上同调的2-上循环引入量子不变量框架，统一并推广现有的不变量理论。

Method: 作者沿两条量子不变量的主要路径展开理论：迹构造（trace constructions）和（余）配对（co-pairings）。利用Yang-Baxter上同调的2-上循环定义量子上循环不变量，并通过高阶形式Laurent多项式形变来推导Jones多项式和Alexander多项式。

Result: 证明了quandle上循环不变量可以在该框架中得到解释；在若干具体例子中（包括基于Kauffman bracket的例子），量子上循环不变量比传统不变量更强；Jones多项式和Alexander多项式均可作为由Yang-Baxter上同调产生的高阶形式Laurent多项式形变不变量在该框架中导出。

Conclusion: 本文建立了一个基于Yang-Baxter上同调2-上循环和形变理论的统一量子不变量框架，不仅统一了quandle上循环不变量，还能产生更强的新不变量，并自然还原Jones和Alexander多项式，为量子链环不变量提供了新的理论视角。

Abstract: Yang-Baxter operators (YBOs) have been employed to construct quantum knot invariants. More recently, cohomology theories for YBOs have been independently developed, drawing inspiration from analogous theories for quandles and other discrete algebraic structures. Quandle cohomology, in particular, gives rise to cocycle invariants for knots via $2$-cocycles, which are closely related to quandle extensions, and knotted surfaces via $3$-cocycles. These quandle cocycle knot invariants have also been shown to admit interpretations as quantum invariants. Similarly, $2$-cocycles in Yang-Baxter cohomology can be interpreted in terms of deformations of YBOs. Building on these parallels, we introduce quantum cocycle invariants of knots using $2$-cocycles of Yang-Baxter cohomology, from the perspective of deformation theory. In particular, we demonstrate that the quandle cocycle invariant can be interpreted in this framework, while the quantum version yields stronger invariants in certain examples. We develop our theory along two primary approaches to quantum invariants: via trace constructions and through (co)pairings. Explicit examples are provided, including those based on the Kauffman bracket. Furthermore, we show that both the Jones and Alexander polynomials can be derived within this framework as invariants arising from higher-order formal Laurent polynomial deformations via Yang-Baxter cohomology.

</details>
