### [1] [The $q$-extension of iterated integrals and nested sums in quantum field theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02702)
*J. Blümlein,A. M. Gavrilik,O. Mykhailiv,C. Schneider*

Main category: math-ph

TL;DR: 本文系统性地构建了微扰量子场论中出现的特殊函数和嵌套求和的q-扩展。这些函数涵盖了从多重对数、Nielsen积分、迭代积分（涉及线性分母、分圆字母、二次型、平方根字母等）到各类嵌套调和和（广义调和和、分圆调和和、含中心二项式系数的嵌套和等）的广泛类别。作者在q自由情形下建立了这些函数及其Mellin变换的q-扩展，并推导了相应的微分和差分方程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着微扰量子场论计算阶数的增加，需要处理的特殊函数类型日益复杂（从多重对数到迭代积分再到更一般的函数）。与此同时，在q形变对易关系的物理背景下，需要这些函数的q-扩展版本，但目前缺乏系统性的构造方法，尤其是对于更复杂字母表的q-扩展。

Method: 论文针对不同复杂度层次的函数空间进行分类处理：对于较简单的函数空间直接给出闭合形式的q-扩展解；对于更复杂的字母表，提供逐步的算法化构造方法。同时推导这些高阶超越函数所满足的微分方程和差分方程，并明确指出q-扩展与μ-扩展之间存在本质区别。

Result: 成功构造了多种特殊函数和嵌套求和的q-扩展，包括多重对数、Nielsen积分、各类迭代积分对应的q-扩展形式，以及嵌套调和和、广义调和和、分圆调和和和含中心二项式系数的嵌套和的q-扩展。对简单情况给出了封闭解，对复杂情况给出了算法化步骤，并推导了满足的微分和差分方程。

Conclusion: 这些q-扩展的特殊函数预计在q形变对易关系下的微扰计算中发挥重要作用，并且q-扩展函数与μ-扩展函数在本质上是不同的，意味着需要单独发展相应的理论工具。

Abstract: Analytic calculations of zero- and single-scale quantities in perturbative quantum field theory result into special numbers and functions, the first of which have been revealed during the last decades. These are generalizations of the polylogarithm in form of Kummer-Poincaré iterative integrals over special alphabets and extensions thereof.With growing order in the coupling constant, the polylogarithms, Nielsen integrals, the iterated integrals over linear denominator terms, cyclotomic letters, letters induced by quadratic forms, square-root valued letters, and more general functions contribute. For the nested sums we consider nested harmonic sums, generalized harmonic sums, nested sums implied by quadratic forms, cyclotomic harmonic sums, and nested sums containing central binomials. We construct the $q$-extensions of these special functions and of the nested sums, which are associated to them by the series expansion at $x=0$, and their Mellin transform in the $q$-free case. These functions are expected to play a role in perturbative calculations in the case of $q$-deformed commutation relations. For the simpler function spaces closed form solutions are presented. For more involved alphabets we present the algorithmic steps leading to the $q$-extension for the individual cases. We also derive the determining differential and difference equations of these higher transcendental functions. The $q$-extended special functions are quite different form the corresponding $μ$-extended functions.

*B. Greenfeld,L. Vendramin,S. Venkatesh*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文继续通过点模块系统研究Nichols代数，确定了所有已知有限维Nichols代数（由群上的简单Yetter-Drinfeld模产生）的截断点空间，发现所有此类代数的3-截断点模块空间为空，并进一步研究了仿射栅栏和特定非平凡栅栏的截断点模块。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Nichols代数是量子代数中最重要且神秘的数学对象之一，需要通过点模块对其进行系统研究以深入理解其结构。

Method: 通过对栅栏（racks）和2-上循环相关的Nichols代数进行截断点模块的系统研究，确定已知有限维Nichols代数的截断点空间，并对其他重要族（如仿射栅栏和大小为3的非平凡不可分解栅栏）进行完整分类。

Conclusion: 本文推进了通过点模块研究Nichols代数的系统计划，为有限维Nichols代数的分类和结构理解提供了新的重要结果。

Abstract: Nichols algebras are among the most important and mysterious objects in quantum algebra. We continue the systematic program of studying them through their point modules. We study truncated point modules of Nichols algebras associated with racks and 2-cocycles over non-abelian groups. We determine the truncated point spaces for all currently known finite-dimensional Nichols algebras arising from simple Yetter-Drinfeld modules over groups. In particular, for all such algebras the space of 3-truncated point modules is empty. We further find all truncated point modules for other important families, including affine racks equipped with the constant 2-cocycle -1, and for the non-trivial indecomposable rack of size three with the constant 2-cocycle a cubic root of one.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 利用 Kinnear 的 skein 模分解框架，将空 skein 直和项的维数归结为周期性 Nielsen 数与 Weyl 共不变格上挠元的可见 Nielsen 数；对 $N=3$ 推导显式公式，并通过构造满足相同周期 Nielsen 数据但不同 $\mathrm{SL}_3$ 维数的环面丛对来验证修正项的必要性。

Abstract: Let $M_γ=T^2\times_γS^1$, where $γ\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is hyperbolic. Its dimension is expressed in terms of the periodic Nielsen numbers $N_k=|\det(I-γ^k)|$ and the visible Nielsen numbers $V_{e\mathbb{Z}^2}(f_γ)$ carried by torsion in the Weyl coinvariant lattices. Only moduli $e\mid N$ occur, so the rank-$N$ summand is determined by $N_1,\ldots,N_N$ together with the visible Nielsen numbers at the divisors of $N$. On the $\mathrm{GL}_N$ permutation lattice these coinvariants are torsion-free, so no such correction occurs; the observer corrections arise precisely upon passage to the $\mathrm{SL}_N$ character lattice. We do not compute the additional endomorphism-algebra summands of the full $\mathrm{SL}_N$-skein module.
