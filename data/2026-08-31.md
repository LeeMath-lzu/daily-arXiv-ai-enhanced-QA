### [1] [Modular Functors with Singularities from Vertex Operator Algebras Beyond Rigidity and Finiteness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28579)
*Lukas Müller,Lukas Woike*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文针对顶点算子代数V及其模的合适范畴，提出了一种构建具有奇点的开闭模函子中形变块空间的方法。该方法受全纯因子化原理启发，利用Costello引入的模扩展策略，为每个具有边界组件的曲面构造映射类群的表示，并证明其满足切除性、C₂-余有限情形下有限维性，且推广了Brochier-Jordan的曲面辫群表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望从全纯因子化原理出发，构建比基于代数几何或拓扑手术的方法更基础、适用范围更广的形变块空间构造。现有构造在理性、刚性、自对偶和有限性等条件下受限，作者希望突破这些限制。

Method: 采用Costello引入的模扩展策略（并在作者前期工作中进一步发展），为每个至少含一个边界组件的曲面Σ及指定的边界标记，构造映射类群的表示Ω_V(Σ;-)。该构造可显式地通过生成Dehn扭转来描述。发展了一种具有奇点的模函子概念，以处理Gaberdiel-Runkel-Wood引入的边界条件。

Result: 在V的模范畴为（不必半单的）模范畴A的特殊情形下，Ω_V(Σ)等价于A的弦网空间，从而等价于Drinfeld中心Z(A)的模函子。该构造超越了理性、刚性、自共轭和有限性条件的限制。证明了Ω_V满足切除性，在C₂-余有限情形下有限维，并能生成推广Brochier-Jordan结果的曲面辫群表示。对三元组W_{2,3}（具有非精确融合积）证明了Gaberdiel-Runkel-Wood边界条件能产生关联函数。

Conclusion: 该论文提供了一种直接从全纯因子化原理出发构建模函子的新途径，其适用范围比现有方法更广（不依赖代数几何或拓扑手术），且在非半单模范畴和非精确融合积情形下仍然有效，为形变块理论和弦网模型的联系提供了新的视角。

Abstract: For a vertex operator algebra $V$ and a suitable category of its modules, we propose a construction for spaces of conformal blocks organized into an open-closed modular functor with singularities. This is inspired by the idea of implementing directly from the start the principle of holomorphic factorization. More precisely, using the strategy of modular extension introduced by Costello and developed further in our previous work, we build for each surface $Σ$ with at least one boundary component per path component and specified boundary labels attached to marked intervals or boundary circles a representation $Ω_V(Σ;-)$ of the mapping class group of $Σ$. The construction can be described explicitly on generating Dehn twists. This approach is a priori independent from other constructions based on algebraic geometry or topological techniques involving e.g. surgery, but we include an overview over the available comparisons. In the special case in which the module category of $V$ is a not necessarily semisimple modular category $\mathcal{A}$, the spaces $Ω_V(Σ)$ are equivalent to the string-net spaces for $\mathcal{A}$ and hence to the modular functor for the Drinfeld center $Z(\mathcal{A})\simeq \bar{\mathcal{A}}\boxtimes\mathcal{A}$. However, the construction of $Ω_V$ in this paper has the advantage of being available beyond rationality, rigidity, self-contragredience and finiteness. Moreover, we prove that $Ω_V$ satisfies excision, is finite-dimensional in the $C_2$-cofinite case and produces representations of surface braid groups generalizing the ones of Brochier-Jordan. We prove for the triplet $\mathcal{W}_{2,3}$ with non-exact fusion product that the boundary conditions introduced by Gaberdiel-Runkel-Wood produce correlation functions, provided that one uses the notion of a modular functor with singularities that we develop.

</details>

### [2] [Augmented Star Products and their Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28220)
*Naoya Miyazaki*

Main category: math-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个广义的星积框架，该框架在保持结合性的同时不必满足标准对易关系，并在此框架下构建了增强星积（augmented star product）及相关星指数，展示了其在特殊函数和量子物理问题中的应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的星积框架通常要求满足标准对易关系，这限制了其在更广泛数学和物理场景中的适用性。作者希望构建一个更通用的星积框架，在保留结合性的同时放宽对易关系的约束，从而拓展星积理论的应用范围。

Method: 作者首先定义了一个广义的星积框架，要求其满足结合性但不强制满足标准对易关系。在此基础上，他们构造了增强星积（augmented star product），并研究了相关的增强星指数（augmented star exponentials）的性质。

Result: 作者成功地将增强星积和增强星指数应用于特殊函数理论以及量子物理中的若干问题，展示了该广义框架的实际效用。

Conclusion: 该广义星积框架为处理不满足标准对易关系的代数结构提供了新工具，在特殊函数和量子物理领域具有潜在的应用价值。

Abstract: In this paper, we introduce a generalized framework for star products that preserve associativity while not necessarily obeying the canonical commutation relations. Within this framework, we formulate the augmented star product and investigate the associated augmented star exponentials. We further demonstrate several applications of these constructions to special functions and to problems arising in quantum physics.

### [3] [Hopf $2$-cocycles for certain affine algebraic reductive groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18599)
*Shlomo Gelaki*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究仿射代数约化群G（其单位分支为环面T）的表示范畴Rep(G)的有限不可分解半单模范畴的分类问题。作者将Rep(G)实现为Rep(T)的K等变化范畴，并通过等变化模范畴数据来刻画其模范畴，进一步利用秩一判据参数化了Rep(G)上的纤维函子，证明所有纤维函子都是经典的（即来自Hopf 2-上循环），并分类了O(G)上的（极小）Hopf 2-上循环。对于交换直积G=T×K，还给出了规范类群的Künneth分解。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 该研究的动机在于理解仿射代数约化群的表示范畴在张量范畴框架下的结构。特别地，如何描述Rep(G)的有限不可分解半单模范畴（对应于该表示范畴的"几何"结构），以及如何分类纤维函子（刻画量子群的遗忘与辫结构），是张量范畴理论中的核心问题。此前类似工作多针对有限群或环面，本文将其推广到更一般的约化群。

Method: 本文将Rep(G)实现为Rep(T)^K的等变化化范畴。对于等变化化的模范畴，以底层Rep(T)-模范畴上的作用传递性作为一个秩一判据，并通过对点稳定子上的射影上循环的非退化性来刻画秩一情形。利用此判据参数化纤维函子，并建立纤维函子与Hopf 2-上循环之间的对应关系。对于交换直积G=T×K的情形，给出规范类群的Künneth分解，包括混合分量，并与分类结果进行对比。

Result: 该文证明了Rep(G)上的每个纤维函子都是经典的，即来源于O(G)上的Hopf 2-上循环；进而分类了O(G)上的（极小）Hopf 2-上循环。对于G=T×K的交换直积情形，给出了规范类群的Künneth分解（含混合分量），并与通过等变化化方法得到的分类结果一致。

Conclusion: 本文建立了仿射代数约化群（单位分支为环面）的表示范畴模范畴与等变化模范畴数据之间的对应关系，给出了秩一的判定准则，并完整分类了纤维函子（皆为经典）及相应的Hopf 2-上循环。该工作将有限群情形的相关理论推广到连续代数群情形，为理解这些表示范畴的张量结构提供了系统的方法。

Abstract: Let $G$ be an affine algebraic reductive group over $\mathbb{C}$ whose identity component is a torus $T$, and let $K:=G/T$. We realize $\Rep(G)$ as an equivariantization $\Rep(T)^K$ and describe its finite indecomposable semisimple module categories in terms of equivariant module-category data over $\Rep(T)$. For such an equivariantization, rank one is characterized by transitivity of the induced action on the simple objects of the underlying $\Rep(T)$-module category, and nondegeneracy of a projective cocycle on a point stabilizer. We use this criterion to parametrize fiber functors on $\Rep(G)$, prove that every such fiber functor is classical, hence arises from a Hopf $2$-cocycle on $\mathscr{O}(G)$, and classify (minimal) Hopf $2$-cocycle on $\mathscr{O}(G)$. For commutative direct products $G=T\times K$, we also give the canonical Künneth decomposition of the group of gauge classes, including the mixed component, and compare it with our classification.

</details>

### [4] [Odd Khovanov homology and higher representation theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.14394)
*Léo Schelstraete,Pedro Vaz*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文引入了$\mathfrak{gl}_2$-foams的超类似物，用于定义定向链环（tangles）的不变量，并证明该不变量在限制到链环时与奇数Khovanov同调一致。作者进一步定义了level 2的$q$-Schur代数的超范畴化，并将其构造实现为某种超-2-表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 旨在为奇数Khovanov同调提供一种表示论层面的构造，使得符号（signs）自然地作为超-2-范畴结构的副产品出现，而非人为添加。

Method: 引入$\mathfrak{gl}_2$-foams的超类似物来定义定向链环的不变量；定义level 2 $q$-Schur代数的超范畴化；将构造实现为超-2-表示；定义超-2-范畴中链复形的张量积，并使其与同伦相容。

Result: 证明所定义的链环不变量与奇数Khovanov同调一致；建立了超-2-表示框架下的表示论构造；定义了具有独立意义的超-2-范畴中链复形张量积。

Conclusion: 该工作给出了奇数Khovanov同调的表示论构造，其中符号自然地源于超-2-范畴结构，同时引入的链复形张量积可能具有更广泛的独立应用价值。

Abstract: We introduce a super analogue of $\mathfrak{gl}_2$-foams, and use it to define an invariant of oriented tangles, shown to coincide with odd Khovanov homology when restricted to links. We then define a supercategorification of the $q$-Schur algebra of level 2 and realize our construction as a certain super-2-representation. This gives a representation-theoretic construction of odd Khovanov homology, where signs naturally arise as a byproduct of the super-2-categorical structure. In the process, we define a tensor product for chain complexes in super-2-categories, suitably compatible with homotopies. This could be of independent interest.

</details>

### [5] [Quantum Information at Infinity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27498)
*L. Barbieri-Viale*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出"量子无穷信息空间"Q∞，通过N级量子空间的逆向极限构造纯态pro-variety，并将其与经典Fisher-Rao统计流形通过黎曼等距对应起来，在adic拓扑下实现了海森堡不确定性原理的正则化，并通过Jordan乘积导出开放系统的Lindblad主方程，系统演化指向绝对零元素作为通用吸引子。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统量子力学框架中，海森堡不确定性原理缺乏结构性的推导与正则化处理，且闭合与开放量子系统的数学描述之间存在框架割裂。作者试图建立一个统一的无穷维架构，使不确定性原理、酉演化与耗散演化能够在同一几何框架内自然涌现。

Method: 采用逆向极限（inverse limit）构造Q∞，基于形式幂级数的adic拓扑；利用实的radical理想直系统的代数余极限驱动N级纯态variety的逆向极限；内化虚数单位，将度量景观压缩为黎曼等距；用adic代数求导刻画不确定性截断阻碍；闭合系统演化作为pro-variety的内蕴导数，开放系统通过非结合对称Jordan乘积导出GKSL型主方程。

Result: 不确定性原理在无穷处表现为受adic代数求导支配的局域截断阻碍；Liouville-von Neumann方程作为切于pro-variety的内蕴导数保持一致性；开放系统的耗散流作为收缩径向向量场，压平高阶jet构型，使态轨迹沿层级树指向绝对零元素的非奇异通用吸引子。

Conclusion: 该框架表明，量子系统在无穷极限下与经典Fisher-Rao统计流形等距同构，闭合与开放演化动力学统一于同一代数-几何结构之中，耗散被解释为层级树上的径向收缩，为量子经典对应及开放量子系统动力学提供了新的数学基底。

Abstract: We introduce the Quantum Information Space at Infinity (Quinfinity) $\mathcal{Q}_\infty$ as the inverse limit of the symbolic Quantum $N$-Spaces $\mathcal{Q}_N$, identified with the complete local real algebra of formal power series under the adic topology. The pure state pro-variety is formalized as the inverse limit of $N$-level pure state varieties, driven by the algebraic colimit of a direct system of real radical ideals. This real architecture internalizes the imaginary unit, collapsing the metric landscapes onto an invariant Riemannian isometry, which identifies the pro-variety directly with the classical Fisher-Rao statistical manifold. Within this framework, the Heisenberg uncertainty principle undergoes a structural regularization at infinity being intrinsically manifested as a localized truncation obstruction governed by adic algebraic derivations. Concurrently, the continuous Liouville-von Neumann equation operates as an internal derivation tangent to the pro-variety, while the open Gorini-Kossakowski-Sudarshan-Lindblad asymptotic master equation is intrinsically obtained via a non-associative symmetric Jordan product. This dissipative flow acts as a contracting radial vector field that dampens higher-order jet configurations, driving the state trajectories down the hierarchical tree toward the absolute zero element as a non-singular universal attractor.

</details>
