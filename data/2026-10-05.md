*Masaki Fukuda,Tommy Shu*

Main category: math.CT

TL;DR: 本文证明了作者新定义的3-交叉模范畴与Gray型4-群范畴等价，从而为Gray 4-群提供了具体的群论计算工具。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Arvasi等人最初为刻画同伦4-型而引入3-交叉模，但其与高阶范畴的关系一直不明确；此前仅有2-交叉模与Gray 3-群的对应关系（Sarikaya–Ulualan）。作者在前作中提出新的3-交叉模定义并观察到可用高阶范畴自然解释，本文旨在将此解释严格化。

In our previous paper, we proposed a new definition of a 3-crossed module and observed that it admits a natural interpretation in terms of higher categories. Our equivalence establishes 3-crossed modules as a concrete, group-theoretic calculus for Gray 4-groups, providing a powerful computational tool for studying surface knots, state-sum invariants, and higher gauge theories.

### [6] [Quantum Graph Theory by Example](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23651)
*Gian Luca Spitzer,Ion Nechita*

Main category: math.OA

TL;DR: 本文提出了一类由矩阵三元组(A,B,C)参数化的非平凡量子图族，可离散化描述并适用图解形式体系，揭示了量子图结构的经典与纯量子分解，并给出若干图参数的精确公式或界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子图由Duan、Severini和Winter引入以刻画量子信道的零差错行为，但量子图不再是离散对象，导致构造有洞见的非平凡例子十分困难，缺乏可分析计算的实例。

Method: 作者构造了一类可在离散意义下理解、并可用Musto、Reutter和Verdon的图解形式体系表达的量子图，这些例子来自逐步缩小的经典矩阵群作用，并由矩阵三元组(A,B,C)参数化。

Result: 该参数化将量子图结构清晰分解为经典分量与纯量子分量：A和C由称为strange graph的经典带权图描述，B提供无经典对应的纯量子贡献；基于此模型给出了连通分量数、色数、独立数和团数等量子图参数的精确公式或界。

Since then, quantum graph theory has become a field of study in its own right. A substantial source of difficulty in working with quantum graphs compared to classical graphs stems from the fact that they are no longer discrete objects. This makes it generally difficult to construct insightful, non-trivial examples. We present a collection of non-trivial quantum graphs that can be thought of in discrete terms, and that can be expressed in the diagrammatic formalism introduced by Musto, Reutter, and Verdon. The examples arise as the quantum graphs acted on by increasingly smaller classical matrix groups, and are parametrised by triples of matrices $(A, B, C)$. The parametrisation reveals a clean decomposition of quantum graph structure into classical and genuinely quantum components: $A$ and $C$ are described by a classical weighted graph called the strange graph, while $B$ provides a purely quantum contribution with no classical analogue. Based on this model, we give exact formulas or establish bounds for quantum graph parameters, such as the number of connected components, the chromatic number, the independence number, and the clique number. Our results provide the first large, parametric families of quantum graphs for which standard graph parameters can be computed analytically.

### [7] [Exact Recovery for Non-Abelian Surface Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03677)
*Alison Warman,Nathanan Tantivasadakarn,Sakura Schafer-Nameki*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究基于任意有限群 $G$ 的量子双重 $D(G)$ 的非阿贝尔拓扑表面码的精确恢复问题，提出了一种正交错误基与确定性恢复协议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 非阿贝尔拓扑表面码（基于量子双重 $D(G)$）的错误恢复比阿贝尔情形更复杂，缺乏完整、正交的错误基描述，且闭合环路错误与稳定子之间存在冗余，导致难以实现精确恢复。

Method: 1) 确定由群乘法算符与不可约表示算符构成的正交错误基；2) 针对晶格或对偶晶格闭合环路错误引起的基冗余（源于稳定子），引入规范固定（gauge-fixing）方法，在码空间上获得完备正交错误基；3) 假定输入为预先确定的中性可纠错误簇，构造电荷转移电路与磁通转移电路，将错误移动到辅助量子比特上并投影消除。

Result: 得到码空间上完备正交的错误基，并构建出电荷与磁通转移电路，实现将错误转移到辅助比特后投影剔除的恢复流程。

Conclusion: 该协议是精确且确定性的恢复协议，适用于任意有限群 $G$（尤其包括非阿贝尔群）的表面码，为非阿贝尔拓扑量子纠错提供了可行方案。

Abstract: We study exact recovery for non-Abelian topological surface codes based on the quantum double $D(G)$ of any finite group $G$. We determine an orthogonal error basis, that is comprised of group multiplication and irreducible representation operators. Whenever the errors are supported on closed loops on the lattice or dual lattice, there is a redundancy in the basis, due to stabilizers. To remedy this, we introduce a gauge-fixing that results in a complete and orthogonal error basis on the code space. Assuming as input predetermined, neutral correctable error clusters, we construct charge and flux transfer circuits that move the errors onto ancillas that then get projected out. This is an exact and deterministic recovery protocol, applicable to surface codes for any finite, in particular non-Abelian, group $G$.

</details>
