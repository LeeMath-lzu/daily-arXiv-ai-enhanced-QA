### [1] [The motivic Lie algebra embeds into the cohomology of the general linear group](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29671)
*Francis Brown,Erik Panzer,Jean-Luc Portner*

Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文证明了混合 Tate 动机的动机李代数典范地嵌入到 GL_g(Z) 局部对称空间的不稳定紧支上同调，以及 Ag（主极化阿贝尔簇模空间）的权零紧支上同调中。构造通过热带几何和图复形实现，并利用单值周期理论证明这些余圈映射到动机李代数的生成元。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将动机李代数与算术群局部对称空间及阿贝尔簇模空间的上同调建立联系，通过热带几何和图复形给出具体构造，并验证这些构造确实生成动机李代数。

Method: 通过热带 Torelli 映射将 Borel 类的紧支类比拉回到典范图余圈；结合 Ross–Willwacher 的结果与单值周期理论，证明这些余圈映射到动机李代数的生成元；并对所有边数不超过14的图计算了典范图余圈。

Result: 建立了动机李代数到局部对称空间和 Ag 紧支上同调的典范嵌入；验证了相关余圈对应动机李代数生成元；完成了所有 ≤14 条边图的典范图余圈计算。

Conclusion: 该工作通过热带几何将动机李代数与几何上同调对象联系起来，为研究混合 Tate 动机的结构提供了新的几何途径。

Abstract: We show that the motivic Lie algebra of mixed Tate motives over $\mathbb{Z}$ embeds canonically into the unstable compactly-supported cohomology of locally symmetric spaces for $\mathrm{GL}_g(\mathbb{Z})$, and into the weight-zero compactly-supported cohomology of $\mathcal{A}_g$, the moduli space of principally polarized abelian varieties. Our construction passes through tropical geometry and graph complexes: compactly-supported analogues of the Borel classes pull back via the tropical Torelli map to canonical graph cocycles. The latter were recently identified with cocycles studied previously by Rossi and Willwacher. We combine their results with the theory of single-valued periods to conclude that the cocycles map to generators of the motivic Lie algebra. We also compute the canonical graph cocycles for all graphs with $\leq14$ edges.

### [2] [Left semibraces, near left semibraces and the Yang-Baxter equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10266)
*Shoufeng Wang*

Main category: math.GR

TL;DR: 本文引入了近左半brace（near left semibrace）的概念，证明了左半brace和近左半brace的加法半群均为矩形群，给出了强左半brace的新刻画，并证明了强左半brace与近左半brace可相互诱导。进一步，利用广义匹配积获得了所有左半brace的结构定理，并给出了与Yang-Baxter方程相关的映射成为集合论解的一个充要条件。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 左半brace和强左半brace已被广泛研究，但其加法半群的结构仍然神秘不清。本文旨在推广近左brace的概念，引入近左半brace，从而揭示左半brace的加法半群结构，并丰富与此相关的Yang-Baxter方程解的研究。

Method: 首先定义近左半brace（四元组(S,+,·,μ)），证明其加法半群为矩形群；其次证明强左半brace与近左半brace之间的相互诱导关系；然后利用广义匹配积建立左半brace的结构定理，将其分解为右零左半brace与右消去左半brace的乘积；最后引入一个新的映射，给出其为Yang-Baxter方程集合论解的充要条件。

Result: 证明了左半brace与近左半brace的加法半群均为矩形群；获得了强左半brace的新刻画；证明了强左半brace可诱导近左半brace，反之亦然；利用广义匹配积得到所有左半brace的结构定理；给出了与近左半brace相关的新映射成为Yang-Baxter方程解的充要条件。

Conclusion: 本文的结果改进和丰富了Jespers与Van Antwerpen、Catino-Colazzo-Stefanelli以及Catino-Mazzotta-Stefanelli等人的已有研究成果，为左半brace和Yang-Baxter方程的研究提供了新的视角和工具。

Abstract: A triple $(S, +, \cdot)$ is called a {\em left semibrace} if $(S, +)$ is a semigroup, $(S, \cdot)$ is a group with identity $e$ and $x(y+z)=(xy)+(x(x^{-1}+z))$ for all $x, y, z\in S$, where $x^{-1}$ is the inverse of $x$ in the group $(S, \cdot)$. A left semibrace $(S,+,\cdot)$ is called {\em strong} if $x+y(e+z)=x+yz$ for all $x, y, z\in S$. Left semibraces and strong left semibraces are investigated extensively in literature. However, the structure of additive semigroups of general left semibraces still remains mysterious. In this note, as generalizations of near left braces, we introduce {\em near left semibraces} as follows. A quadruple $(S,+, \cdot, μ)$ is called a {\em near left semibrace} if $(S, +)$ is a semigroup, $(S, \cdot)$ is a group, $μ: S\to S$ is a map and $x(y+z)=(xy)+μ(x)+xz$ for all $x, y, z\in S$. We first show that the additive semigroups of both left semibrace and near left semibraces are rectangular groups and obtain some new characterizations of strong left semibraces. In particular, we prove that a strong left semibrace can induce a near left semibrace, and vice versa. As a consequence, near left semibraces can provide set-theoretical solutions for the Yang-Baxter equation. Next, we obtain a structure theorem for all left semibraces by the generalized matched products of right zero left semibraces and right cancellative left semibraces. Finally, we consider a new map associated to a near left semibrace and give a sufficient and necessary condition under which such a map forms a set-theoretic solution of the Yang-Baxter equation. Our result improve and enrich some results obtained by Jespers and Van Antwerpen in [Forum Math. 31 (2019) 241--263], by Catino, Colazzo and Stefanelli in [Mediterr. J. Math. 17 (2020) 58] and by Catino, Mazzotta and Stefanelli in [J. Algebra 573 (2021) 576--619].

### [3] [Trace radicals and cocenters of free products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24697)
*Nikita Safonkin*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究了单位结合代数的"迹残余有限维"（trace RFD）性质，证明了两个RFD代数的自由积$A*B$的迹根等于$A$和$B$迹根的直和，从而$A*B$是trace RFD当且仅当$A$和$B$都是trace RFD。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在算子代数和非交换几何中，有限维表示和迹泛函是理解代数结构的重要工具。作者引入"迹残余有限维"的概念，希望将RFD性质从表示层面推广到余中心（cocenter）层面，并探究该性质在自由积操作下是否保持。

Method: 证明分为两个关键部分：一是对自由积$A*B$的余中心进行显式分解；二是构造有限维表示，使其迹能够检测长度大于1的词的非零类，从而分离余中心中的元素。

Result: 两个RFD代数$A$和$B$的自由积$A*B$的迹根等于各自迹根的直和。由此推出：$A*B$是trace RFD当且仅当$A$和$B$都是trace RFD。

Conclusion: 该结果建立了trace RFD性质在自由积下的充要条件，为研究代数的有限维表示与迹结构提供了新的工具，也对理解自由积的表示理论有重要意义。

Abstract: We call a unital associative algebra $A$ trace residually finite-dimensional if its elements are separated by finite-dimensional representations and its cocenter $A/[A,A]$ is separated by the corresponding trace functionals. For RFD algebras $A$ and $B$, we prove that the trace radical of $A*B$, the common kernel of all finite-dimensional trace functionals, is the direct sum of the trace radicals of $A$ and $B$, which implies that $A*B$ is trace RFD if and only if both $A$ and $B$ are trace RFD. The proof combines an explicit cocenter decomposition with a construction of finite-dimensional representations whose traces detect nonzero classes of words of length greater than one.

### [6] [Non-Invertible Symmetries Mixing with Witt Non-Trivial Quantum Cellular Automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21698)
*Kansei Inamura,Oskar Wojdel,Lukasz Fidkowski,Sakura Schafer-Nameki*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究3+1维Z_p 1-form对称性的自对偶性（Kramers-Wannier-Wegner对偶S）与SPT堆叠操作T所构成的群结构。在连续统中，这些操作形成中心扩张群（p=2时为PSL(2,Z_4)的中心扩张，奇素数为SL(2,Z_p)的中心扩张），其中心元是纯引力响应的可逆理论。作者确定了一个扭曲的分级Witt群泛化来表征这些中心扩张，并将其微观实现为量子细胞自动机（QCA）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 作者利用扭曲的分级Witt群泛化（abelian任意子理论）来确定中心扩张结构；随后将整个结构微观实现为量子细胞自动机（QCA），作用于立方晶格上自旋格点Hilbert空间的局部算子代数。该代数由与Z_p 1-form对称性对易的局部算子构成，并除以1-form对称性生成元。

Specifically, our local operator algebra is built by starting with all local operators commuting with a $\mathbb{Z}_p$ 1-form symmetry, and taking the quotient by all the (local) 1-form symmetry generators. The central elements can always be extended to the full tensor product algebra with a uniquely defined QCA class. Consequently the lattice fusion rules reproduce the continuum ones only up to these QCAs and lattice translations, giving rise to fusion rules refined by QCAs.
