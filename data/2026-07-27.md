### [1] [Quantum Cellular Automata from Kramers-Wannier Dualities and Modular Relations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21728)
*Carolyn Zhang,Po-Shen Hsin*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文揭示了引力拓扑响应在量子 cellular automata（QCA）、高维对偶性和晶格模型中的统一作用，建立了有限深度量子电路与QCA制备状态之间的区别，并提出了新的投影SL(2,Z_N)关系及其通过测量和纠错的制备协议。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决引力拓扑项在连续场论中被视为无关紧要的抵消项，但在晶格上却具有本质区别——区分有限深度量子电路（FDQCs）与非平凡量子 cellular automata（QCAs）制备的状态。

Method: 通过分析QCAs与引力拓扑响应的关联，在四个相关场景中建立联系：(1) 投影SL(2,Z_N)关系的晶格实现；(2) 对偶性平方的推广；(3) 高阶规范场论的晶格实现；(4) 广义时间反演对称保护的拓扑相。并给出了利用有限深度幺正电路、测量和纠错制备QCA纠缠态的协议。

Conclusion: 引力拓扑响应不仅仅是连续场论中的抵消项，它们在晶格上具有本质的物理意义，统一了高维非可逆对称性、对偶性和量子 cellular automata 的研究。

Abstract: Recent work has constructed higher-dimensional analogs of non-invertible symmetries similar to 1+1d Kramers-Wannier duality. Although their continuum descriptions often treat purely gravitational topological terms as inessential counterterms, these terms can have an essential lattice manifestation: they distinguish states prepared by finite-depth quantum circuits (FDQCs) from those entangled by nontrivial quantum cellular automata (QCAs). Motivated by this mismatch, we show that QCAs associated with gravitational topological responses arise in several related settings: (1) lattice realizations of projective $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}_N)$ relations generated by topological operations on symmetries; (2) squares of dualities that generalize the relation between fermionization and Kramers-Wannier duality; (3) lattice implementations of QCAs through higher-form gauging; and (4) invertible phases protected by generalized time-reversal symmetries. We derive new projective $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}_N)$ relations whose projective phases are gravitational topological responses constructed from Stiefel-Whitney classes. We furthermore give a general protocol for preparing the associated QCA-entangled states using finite-depth unitary circuits, measurements, and error correction. These results unify the study of gravitational topological responses in field theories, higher dimensional dualities, and quantum cellular automata.

### [2] [Non-Invertible Symmetries Mixing with Witt Non-Trivial Quantum Cellular Automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21698)
*Kansei Inamura,Oskar Wojdel,Lukasz Fidkowski,Sakura Schafer-Nameki*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了3+1维中$\mathbb{Z}_p$ 1-形式对称性的自对偶与SPT相堆叠操作，揭示了其连续理论中的群结构（$p=2$时为$PSL(2,\mathbb{Z}_4)$的中心扩张，奇素数$p$时为$SL(2,\mathbb{Z}_p)$的中心扩张），并证明了这些中心扩张由阿贝尔任意子理论的扭分次Witt群控制。对于$p=2$，所得群为单比特Clifford群。作者进一步在立方晶格上用量子胞自动机（QCA）实现了这一结构，并发现晶格融合规则与连续理论仅相差QCA和晶格平移。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解1-形式对称性下的自对偶性（Kramers-Wannier-Wegner对偶$S$）与SPT相堆叠操作$T$在3+1维连续理论和晶格模型中的代数结构，揭示其与QCA的深刻联系。

Method: 利用群论方法研究连续理论中的代数结构（中心扩张与Witt群），并在晶格上构建局部算子代数，通过量子胞自动机（QCA）实现该结构。

Result: 确定了两种情况下中心扩张的代数结构：$p=2$时对应单比特Clifford群（Hadamard门与相位门）；奇素数$p$时对应$SL(2,\mathbb{Z}_p)$的中心扩张。晶格模型中的融合规则与连续理论相差QCA和晶格平移。

Conclusion: 建立了1-形式对称性对偶操作与SPT堆叠的完整代数框架，揭示了其与QCA的对应关系，为理解高形式对称性的非可逆对偶对称性提供了晶格实现。

Abstract: Self-dualities and the stacking of symmetry-protected topological (SPT) phases are basic operations on quantum many-body systems. For a $\mathbb{Z}_p$ one-form symmetry in 3+1d these correspond to the Kramers-Wannier-Wegner duality $S$, which is the gauging operation underlying non-invertible duality symmetries, and the stacking of a 1-form symmetry SPT $T$. In the continuum, they form a central extension of $PSL(2,\mathbb{Z}_4)$ for $p=2$, and of $SL(2,\mathbb{Z}_p)$ for odd primes $p$, whose central elements are invertible theories with purely gravitational response. These central extensions are governed by a twisted, graded generalization of the Witt group of abelian anyon theories, which we determine. For $p=2$ the resulting group is the single-qubit Clifford group, with duality and entangler acting as the Hadamard and phase gates. We realize this entire structure microscopically as quantum cellular automata (QCA) acting on a certain local operator algebra associated with a spin lattice Hilbert space on a cubic lattice. Specifically, our local operator algebra is built by starting with all local operators commuting with a $\mathbb{Z}_p$ 1-form symmetry, and taking the quotient by all the (local) 1-form symmetry generators. The central elements can always be extended to the full tensor product algebra with a uniquely defined QCA class. For $p=2$ they are generated by the non-trivial semion QCA, and for odd prime $p$ they are generated by the non-trivial $\mathbb{Z}_p$ Clifford QCA. Consequently the lattice fusion rules reproduce the continuum ones only up to these QCAs and lattice translations, giving rise to fusion rules refined by QCAs.

### [3] [Non-Clifford quantum cellular automata from invertible topological quantum field theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21697)
*Meng Sun,Zongyuan Wang,Bowen Yang,Nathanan Tantivasadakarn,Yu-An Chen*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文发展了一种统一的代数构造方法，将拓扑量子场论（TQFT）转化为局域保持的量子胞自动机（QCA），在三维空间中统一了所有已知的与Witt群$\mathbb Z_8\times\mathbb Z_2$子群相关的QCA构造，并在更高维度中得到了新的无限族非Clifford QCA，同时通过显式构造有限深度量子电路证明了某些高维QCA是平凡的。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 从拓扑量子场论构造量子胞自动机是一个挑战：拓扑作用量可以产生实现期望基态的交换哈密顿量，但它本身并不指定完整局域算子代数的自同构。本文旨在填补这一空白，建立从TQFT到微观QCA的系统构造方法。

Method: 提出统一的代数构造方法，将哈密顿量的交换生成子扩展为完整张量积希尔伯特空间上的分离子-翻转子代数；利用Wu类乘积构造任意维度的QCA；通过有限深度量子电路证明某些QCA的平凡性。

Result: 统一了三维中所有与$\mathbb Z_8\times\mathbb Z_2$子群相关的QCA构造；得到$d=4k-1$维的新无限族广义$U(1)_2$和$U(1)_4$非Clifford QCA；得到$w_2^nw_3^m$（$d=2n+3m-1$）和$w_2w_{4k-1}$（$d=4k$）两类无限族QCA；证明了5维$w_3^2$和$w_2^3$ QCA是平凡的。

Conclusion: 该工作将可逆TQFT转化为微观QCA，提供了超越Clifford设置的高维构造可扩展路径，为分类QCA的稳定结构和边界反常开辟了系统方法。

Abstract: Quantum cellular automata (QCAs) describe locality-preserving quantum dynamics and connect quantum information, many-body physics, and topological quantum field theory (TQFT). Constructing a QCA from a TQFT, however, is challenging. Although a topological action can produce a commuting Hamiltonian realizing the desired ground state, it does not by itself specify an automorphism of the full local operator algebra. In this work, we develop a unified algebraic construction that extends the commuting generators of the Hamiltonian to a complete separator-flipper algebra on the full tensor-product Hilbert space, providing a microscopic definition of the corresponding QCA. In three spatial dimensions, our formalism unifies all previously known QCA constructions associated with the $\mathbb Z_8\times\mathbb Z_2$ subgroup of the Witt group, including the $U(1)_2$ and $U(1)_4$ QCAs. The same algebraic structure directly yields new infinite families of generalized $U(1)_2$ and $U(1)_4$ non-Clifford QCAs in dimensions $d=4k-1$. We also reformulate the 4-dimensional $w_2w_3$ QCA and use it to develop a general construction of QCAs from TQFTs associated with arbitrary products of Wu classes. This construction includes two infinite families. The first consists of $w_2^nw_3^m$ QCAs in dimension $d=2n+3m-1$, while the second consists of $w_2w_{4k-1}$ QCAs in dimension $d=4k$. As a contrasting result, we explicitly construct finite-depth quantum circuits for the 5-dimensional $w_3^2$ and $w_2^3$ QCAs, thereby proving that they are trivial, in agreement with the cobordism classification. Overall, these results convert invertible TQFTs into microscopic QCAs, provide a scalable route to higher-dimensional constructions beyond the Clifford setting, and open a systematic approach to classifying their stable structures and boundary anomalies.

</details>

### [4] [Parafermionizing the Monster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10902)
*Yamato Honda,Justin Kaidi,Ippo Orii*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 该论文研究了Monster CFT关于其ZpA子群的parafermionization，证明其等价于一种非可逆规范，并推导出Monster CFT具有Rep(so(3)_p)对称性，计算了相关缺陷McKay-Thompson级数并证明了其不变子群为Γ_1(p+2)。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解Monster CFT在ZpA子群下的parafermionization结构，揭示其深层对称性。

Method: 通过非可逆规范理论，将parafermionization等价为P(p)×P(p)^∨的规范，利用对称性跟踪和表示论分析。

Result: 证明了对角Monster CFT具有Rep(so(3)_p) ⊠ Rep(so(3)_p)^op对称性，holomorphic Monster理论具有Rep(so(3)_p)对称性，缺陷McKay-Thompson级数的不变子群为Γ_1(p+2)。

Conclusion: 揭示了Monster CFT在奇素数p的ZpA子群下的对称性结构，建立了与parafermion理论和表示论的联系。

Abstract: We study the parafermionization of the Monster CFT with respect to its $\mathbb{Z}_{pA}$ subgroups, with $p$ an odd prime. Under certain assumptions, we show that the parafermionization is equal to a non-invertible gauging of $\mathcal{P}(p) \times \mathcal{P}(p)^\vee$, where $\mathcal{P}(p)$ is the theory of $\mathbb{Z}_p$-parafermions and $\mathcal{P}(p)^\vee$ is an appropriate dual theory, with global symmetry characterized by the centralizer of $\mathbb{Z}_{pA}$. By tracking the symmetries of $\mathcal{P}(p) \times \mathcal{P}(p)^\vee$ through the non-invertible gauging, we argue that the diagonal Monster CFT has $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathfrak{so}(3)_p) \boxtimes \mathrm{Rep}(\mathfrak{so}(3)_p)^\mathrm{op}$ symmetry, and hence that the holomorphic Monster theory has symmetry $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathfrak{so}(3)_p)$. We then compute the defect McKay-Thompson series associated to these symmetries, and prove that their invariance subgroups are $Γ_1(p+2)$.

### [5] [Nuancing the unicity of $q$-rationals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22308)
*Perrine Jouteur,Olga Paris-Romaskevich,Alexander Thomas*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文利用特征簇证明了q-有理数在共轭意义下的唯一性，同时发现了具有模对称性的两参数变形族，并指出其中两种变形能给出标准q-整数，其中一种新变形具有更好的正性性质且可直接计算有理纽结的琼斯多项式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究q-有理数的唯一性问题，并探索其变形与模对称性，寻找具有更好正性性质且能直接计算琼斯多项式的新表示。

Method: 利用特征簇(character varieties)进行理论证明，通过共轭关系分析变形族。

Result: 证明了q-有理数在共轭意义下的唯一性；发现两参数变形族；确定仅两种变形给出标准q-整数；新变形具有正性性质且可直接计算有理纽结的琼斯多项式。

Conclusion: q-有理数具有唯一性但存在非平凡变形，新变形在正性性质和纽结理论中具有重要应用价值。

Abstract: We prove unicity of $q$-rational numbers up to conjugacy, using character varieties. Despite the unicity, we exhibit a two-parameter family of deformations of rationals with a modular symmetry. We prove that there are exactly two deformations which deliver the usual $q$-integers: the original $q$-rationals defined by Morier-Genoud and Ovsienko, and another new one. Although the new family can be obtained by conjugacy from the old one, new positivity properties appear. In addition, this new family provides a direct computation of the Jones polynomial of rational knots.

</details>
