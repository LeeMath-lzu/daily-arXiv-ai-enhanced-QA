### [1] [Vertex operator algebra realizations of untwisted Haagerup--Izumi centers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32928)
*David E. Evans,Andrew Riesen*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文构造了负球面分支上的非扭转Haagerup–Izumi融合范畴 $\mathcal H_N$（$N\ge 3$ 为奇数），并构造了中心荷为 $-16N$ 的简单、自反、正则、$C_2$-余有限的顶点算子代数 $E_N$，证明 $\operatorname{Rep}(E_N)\simeq Z(\mathcal H_N)$，并给出其完整的ribbon模数据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Haagerup–Izumi 融合范畴的负球面分支缺乏具体的、可实现为 VOA 表示范畴的模型，尤其是与模融合范畴中心对应的构造。作者希望为每个奇数 $N$ 给出显式的 VOA 实现，并识别其模数据。

Method: 先构造中心荷为 $-5N$ 的 CFT 型循环 Virasoro 扩张并证明其正则性；用两次范畴凝聚（condensation）恢复 $\mathcal H_N$；再通过平移格模的有限扩张去除辅助的 pointed 因子，得到 $E_N$。

Result: $E_N$ 是 rational 的，恰有 $N^2+3$ 个不可约模，下界整数分次，最小共形次数 $-(N-1)/2$。完整ribbon模数据等于 $\mathrm{MD}_-(\mathbb Z/N;\mathbb Z/(N^2+4),b\mapsto b^2/(N^2+4))$（Gannon–Schopieray–Yadav 记号），对每个奇数 $N$ 成立。

Conclusion: 负球面分支的 untwisted Haagerup–Izumi 范畴对每个奇数 $N\ge3$ 均可由具体 VOA $E_N$ 实现，其模数据被完全确定，为这些范畴提供了新的 CFT 构造与显式模不变量。

Abstract: For every odd integer $N\geq3$, we construct an untwisted Haagerup--Izumi fusion category $\mathcal H_N$ on the negative spherical branch and a simple, self-contragredient, regular, $C_2$-cofinite vertex operator algebra $E_N$ of central charge $-16N$, together with an equivalence of modular fusion categories: \[
  \operatorname{Rep}(E_N)\simeq Z(\mathcal H_N). \]
  The VOA $E_N$ is rational, has exactly $N^2+3$ irreducible modules, and is lower bounded and integer graded, with least conformal degree $-(N-1)/2$. The proof begins with a CFT-type cyclic Virasoro extension of central charge $-5N$, whose regularity we establish. Two categorical condensations recover $\mathcal H_N$, and a finite extension by shifted lattice modules removes the auxiliary pointed factor. We identify the complete ribbon modular data of $Z(\mathcal H_N)$ with $\mathrm{MD}_{-}(\mathbb Z/N;\mathbb Z/(N^2+4),b\mapsto b^2/(N^2+4))$ in the notation of Gannon--Schopieray--Yadav, for every odd $N$.

### [2] [Ozone groups and centers of two-parameter quantum Heisenberg algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31781)
*James Gómez,Héctor Suárez,Helbert Venegas*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 论文研究了双参数量子Heisenberg代数$\mathcal H_{p,q}$（等价于诺特PI分次down-up代数）：确定其“臭氧群”为$\mu_\ell\times\mu_{\ell/t}$、用臭氧群不动环刻画中心、给出中心作为循环商曲面奇点上的超曲面的表示，并刻画中心正则、Gorenstein及Calabi–Yau的条件。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Chambers–Gaddis–Won–Zhang等人曾猜测中心正则性与某个条件有关（此处被反驳），且双参数量子Heisenberg代数的中心与对称性结构（臭氧群、中心作为不变量环）尚未被系统刻画。作者希望用“臭氧群”这一工具统一处理整类诺特PI分次down-up代数，并厘清正则、Gorenstein、Calabi–Yau等代数性质与参数$p,q$（单位根）的算术关系。

Method: 设$p,q$为单位根且$pq\neq1$，$\ell=|\langle p,q\rangle|$，$t=\ord(pq)$。利用同构分类把问题化归到双参数量子Heisenberg代数$\mathcal H_{p,q}$，引入臭氧群$G=\mu_\ell\times\mu_{\ell/t}$（由三个正规元共轭生成），通过固定环$Z(\mathcal H_{p,q})=\mathcal H_{p,q}^{G}$（等价于$t$阶循环群的不变量）计算中心；进一步给出中心的显式表示，并借助算术条件（$1$是否为特征根；$p^{m/h}=q^{n/h}$，$m=\ord(p),n=\ord(q),h=\gcd(m,n)$）判定正则性与Gorenstein性，同时分析$\mathcal H_{p,q}$的Calabi–Yau性、自同构与局部幂零导子。

Result: (1) 臭氧群为$\mu_\ell\times\mu_{\ell/t}$，中心等于臭氧群固定环$\mathcal H_{p,q}^{G}$，也等于$t$阶循环群的不变量；(2) 中心有“循环商曲面奇点上的超曲面”表示；(3) 中心正则当且仅当$1$是特征根（从而反驳Chan–Gaddis–Won–Zhang的猜想）；(4) 中心Gorenstein当且仅当$p^{m/h}=q^{n/h}$；例如$p=\zeta_5,q=\zeta_5^2$时中心不正则但（对照条件）不Gorenstein；(5) 判定了臭氧群固定环的Gorenstein性以及$\mathcal H_{p,q}$的Calabi–Yau性；(6) 中心正则的代数具有非仿射自同构和非零局部幂零导子。

Conclusion: 双参数量子Heisenberg代数的中心结构可由臭氧群的不动环完全刻画，且正则性、Gorenstein性、Calabi–Yau性均可由参数$p,q$的阶与乘积阶的简单算术条件判定；中心正则的情形对应丰富的自同构与局部幂零导子，说明此类代数在中心正则时具有非交换代数几何意义上的“非刚性”现象。

Abstract: Let $p,q$ be roots of unity with $pq\neq1$, and put $\ell=|\langle p,q\rangle|$ and $t=\ord(pq)$. Up to isomorphism, the two-parameter quantum Heisenberg algebras $\mathcal H_{p,q}$ are exactly the noetherian PI graded down-up algebras, so everything here holds for that whole class. The ozone group is $μ_\ell\timesμ_{\ell/t}$, generated by conjugation by three normal elements, and the center is recovered from the fixed ring of the ozone group as the invariants of a cyclic group of order $t$. We give a presentation of the center as a hypersurface over a cyclic quotient surface singularity. It is regular exactly when $1$ is a characteristic root, which disproves a conjecture of Chan, Gaddis, Won and Zhang, and Gorenstein exactly when $p^{m/h}=q^{n/h}$, where $m=\ord(p)$, $n=\ord(q)$ and $h=\gcd(m,n)$; for $p=ζ_5$, $q=ζ_5^{2}$ it is not. We also decide when the fixed ring of the ozone group is Gorenstein and when $\mathcal H_{p,q}$ is Calabi--Yau, and show that the algebras with regular center have non-affine automorphisms and nonzero locally nilpotent derivations.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Conclusion: 本文完成了Lusztig辫群对称在准分裂i量子群上的综合推广，建立了秩一显式公式框架，并打通了与相对辫群对称、Lusztig对称及整形式层面的联系。

Abstract: We present a comprehensive generalization of Lusztig's braid group symmetries for quasi-split iquantum groups. These symmetries are formulated in terms of idivided powers and iweights of the vectors being acted upon. We show that these symmetries are consistent with the relative braid group symmetries on iquantum groups, and they are also related to Lusztig's symmetries via quasi $K$-matrices. Furthermore, through appropriate rescaling, we obtain compatible symmetries for the integral forms of modified iquantum groups and their integrable modules. *Yaolong Shen,Changjian Su,Zichang Wang,Rui Xiong*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文给出余切型 K-理论量子化 Coulomb 分支代数中准微小单极子算符的公式，并构造从移位仿射 i-量子群到 q-差分算子代数的 GKLO 型同态，证明其穿过量子化 Coulomb 分支代数，从而用“带修饰”的微小与准微小单极子算符几何实现移位仿射 i-量子群。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

These homomorphisms give geometric realizations of the shifted affine $\mathrm{i}$quantum groups in terms of dressed minuscule and quasi-minuscule monopole operators.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: In this paper, we introduce the notion of stable imaginary elements in quantum groups and use crystal basis theory to construct explicit one-parameter families of such elements for all finite types.
