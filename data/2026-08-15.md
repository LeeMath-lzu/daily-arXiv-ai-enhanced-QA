### [1] [On the Cartan Graphs of Nichols Algebras over Coquasi-Hopf Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05065)
*Bowen Li*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文研究了特征零代数闭域上、具有双射对极的余拟Hopf代数H上的有限维简单Yetter-Drinfeld模元组M。证明了若M允许所有反射，则其半Cartan图是Cartan图，并通过张量可分解性刻画了该Cartan图的有限性，获得了Nichols代数的有限维判据。还证明了辫子monoidal等价保持反射及相关Cartan图，并应用于有限交换群上的对角型元组Cartan图的覆盖构造。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Cartan图在Nichols代数研究中具有核心地位，是理解量子群和Hopf代数结构的重要工具。作者旨在建立更广泛的Cartan图理论框架，将其从简单情形推广到一般余拟Hopf代数上的Yetter-Drinfeld模，并探讨其与Nichols代数有限维性之间的关系。

Method: 论文采用代数与范畴论方法，通过研究反射（reflections）在Yetter-Drinfeld模元组上的作用，构造半Cartan图并将其提升为Cartan图。利用张量分解性质刻画Cartan图的有限性，借助辫子monoidal等价来证明反射和Cartan图在该等价下的不变性。对于对角型情形，通过有限交换群上的abélien 3-上圈与平凡结合子的比较构造覆盖关系。

Result: 证明了允许所有反射的元组的半Cartan图是Cartan图；建立了Cartan图有限性与Nichols代数张量可分解性之间的等价刻画，得到Nichols代数有限维性判据；证明了辫子monoidal等价保持反射和Cartan图。还证明了有限交换群上带abélien 3-上圈的对角型类型的Cartan图可由平凡结合子的对角型覆盖，且实根集在对应对象处一致，并构造了一个不能由平凡对角型得到的Cartan图例子。

Conclusion: 该工作系统性地建立了余拟Hopf代数框架下Cartan图理论，深化了对Nichols代数有限维性的理解，并揭示了带3-上圈与平凡结合子情形之间Cartan图的覆盖关系。这表明该框架较已有理论更具一般性和灵活性。

Abstract: Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, let $H$ be a coquasi-Hopf algebra with bijective antipode, and let $M$ be a tuple of finite-dimensional simple Yetter--Drinfeld modules over $H$. We prove that, if $M$ admits all reflections, then its associated semi-Cartan graph is a Cartan graph. We characterize the finiteness of this Cartan graph by tensor decomposability of $\mathcal B(M)$ and obtain a finite-dimensionality criterion of Nichols algebras. We also show that braided monoidal equivalences preserve reflections and the associated Cartan graphs. As applications, we prove that every Cartan graph associated with a diagonal type tuple over a finite abelian group equipped with an abelian \(3\)-cocycle is covered by one arising from a diagonal type tuple over
  some finite abelian group $G$ with trivial associator, and that their real-root sets agree at corresponding objects. We also construct a Cartan graph of the former kind that cannot be obtained from any diagonal tuple in \({}_G^G\mathcal{YD}\).

*V. K. Dobrev*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文探讨Langlands对偶与不变微分算子之间的联系，研究对象为SL(2n+1)群，补充了此前对SL(2n)群的研究，因为二者的表示理论存在显著差异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Langlands纲领与不变微分算子均根植于Harish-Chandra的半单李群表示论，但两者此前看似无关。作者试图在两者之间建立桥梁，填补这一理论空白。

Method: 在已完成的SL(2n)群研究基础上，将分析扩展到SL(2n+1)群的情形，比较两个群的表示理论差异，并建立Langlands对偶与不变微分算子之间的联系。

Result: 尚未在摘要中展示具体结果，本文主要介绍了研究动机和新的研究对象SL(2n+1)，结果有待正文呈现。

Conclusion: 通过研究SL(2n+1)群，作者希望进一步填补Langlands纲领与不变微分算子之间的桥梁，该群与SL(2n)在表示理论上的差异性为这一目标提供了新的视角和挑战。

Abstract: Recently we started building a bridge between two cases of Langlands duality. The latter is one of the most influential topics in mathematical research. It has many different appearances and influential subtopics. Yet there is a topic that until now seems unrelated to the Langlands program. That is the topic of invariant differential operators. That is strange since both items are deeply rooted in Harish-Chandra's representation theory of semisimple Lie groups.
  We started with the case of the group ~$SL(2n)$. In the present paper we deal with the group $SL(2n+1)$. The two mentioned groups are similar, but their representation theories is rather different.

### [3] [Categorification of the genus two DAHA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13278)
*Semeon Arthamonov,Ievgen Makedonskyi,Daniil Sarafannikov*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文构造了平坦退化D(2|1,α)当前代数的分级可积表示的导出范畴上的三个导出自函子，并证明它们在Grothendieck群中的类就是亏格2的Macdonald算子。通过显式计算分级Ext配对，推导出亏格2 Macdonald算子的自伴性以及亏格2 Macdonald多项式在特定斜双线性形式下的正交性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 该研究的动机是将Macdonald算子（亏格2情形）与表示论中的导出范畴工具联系起来。通过构造当前代数的导出自函子，将代数表示论与Macdonald理论的组合/对称函数结构建立桥梁，从而为理解亏格2 Macdonald算子和多项式的性质提供表示论的证明框架。

Method: 首先在平坦退化D(2|1,α)当前代数的分级可积表示的导出范畴上构造三个导出自函子。然后通过计算这些自函子在Grothendieck群中的类，识别出亏格2的Macdonald算子。接着利用显式计算的Ext配对（graded Ext pairing）来研究这些算子的性质，由此推导出算子的自伴性以及多项式的正交性结论。

Result: 成功证明了三个导出自函子在Grothendieck群中的类对应于亏格2 Macdonald算子。通过显式计算分级Ext配对，得到了亏格2 Macdonald算子的自伴性，以及亏格2 Macdonald多项式在特定斜双线性形式下的正交性。

Conclusion: 这项工作建立了当前代数表示论的导出范畴方法与亏格2 Macdonald理论之间的深刻联系，将表征论技术应用于Macdonald算子的结构性质研究中，拓展了Macdonald理论的表示论解释。

Abstract: We construct three derived endofunctors on the derived category of graded integrable representations of the current algebra of a flat degeneration of $D(2|1,α)$, and prove that their classes in the Grothendieck group are the genus two Macdonald operators. Computing the graded $\operatorname{Ext}$ pairing explicitly, we deduce the self-adjointness of the genus two Macdonald operators and the orthogonality of genus $2$ Macdonald polynomials with respect to the certain skew-bilinear form.

</details>
