Method: 利用 GL_N 基本表示下的 Baxter–Belavin 量子 R-矩阵表示两组生成元，其余生成元用 Komori–Hikami 椭圆 K-矩阵表示；通过 Felder–Pasquier 程序重新推导并修正奇数 N 的单位性关系；再由 R-矩阵取值的 Lax 对构造自旋链。

Result: 给出椭圆情形两组生成元的矩阵表示、修正后的奇数 N 单位性关系，构造出 BC_n 型 Calogero–Inozemtsev 系统的长程量子自旋链实例，以及带修正自旋位置的 Haldane–Shastry 与 Polychronakos–Frahm 链。

Conclusion: BC_n 型结合 Yang–Baxter 代数的矩阵表示为构造长程自旋链提供了统一框架，并可推广到外场 Calogero–Moser 情形。

Abstract: We construct a matrix representation of the algebra defined by the associative Yang--Baxter equations of ${\rm BC}_n$ type. In the elliptic case, two sets of generators are represented in terms of the Baxter--Belavin quantum $R$-matrix in the fundamental representation of ${\rm GL}_N$, while the remaining generators are represented by the elliptic $K$-matrix found by Komori and Hikami. We revisit its derivation using the Felder--Pasquier procedure and correct the unitarity relation for odd $N$. We then use these matrix representations to construct examples of long-range quantum spin chains from $R$-matrix-valued Lax pairs for the classical Calogero--Inozemtsev system associated with the ${\rm BC}_n$ root system. We also construct $R$-matrix-valued Lax pairs for Calogero--Moser models in an external field, leading to Haldane--Shastry and Polychronakos--Frahm chains with modified spin positions.
