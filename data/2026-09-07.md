Motivation: 作者旨在揭示shifted Yangians表示范畴的Grothendieck环的代数结构，特别是验证Hernandez-Zhang猜想（Grothendieck环与cluster代数同构）以及Frenkel-Hernandez和Geiss-Hernandez-Leclerc关于扩展QQ-系统的猜想，同时建立Langlands对偶群的作用。

Method: 通过构造一种新型的pro-variety（基于交替堆的开放双无穷Bott-Samelson簇），证明其Cox环与shifted Yangians表示范畴的（复化）Grothendieck环同构；利用Francone-Leclerc的工作识别上述环与Geiss-Hernandez-Leclerc定义的cluster代数；借助shifted余积构造截断shifted Yangians的余积。

Result: 证明了Grothendieck环与开放双无穷Bott-Samelson簇的Cox环同构，验证了Hernandez-Zhang猜想；在Grothendieck环上构造了Langlands对偶群G^∨的作用；证明了Frenkel-Hernandez和Geiss-Hernandez-Leclerc关于扩展QQ-系统的猜想；推广了Hernandez-Leclerc的对偶。

Conclusion: 本文为shifted Yangians表示论与Bott-Samelson几何、cluster代数以及Langlands对偶框架之间建立了深刻联系，证明了一系列重要猜想，为量子群和相关可积系统理论的进一步发展提供了统一视角。

Abstract: In this paper, we study the category $\mathcal{O}$ of representations of shifted Yangians associated to a simply-laced simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ over $\mathbb{C}$. In particular, we prove that the (complexified) Grothendieck ring of this category is isomorphic to the Cox ring of the open bi-infinite Bott-Samelson variety, which is a pro-variety we construct from Bott-Samelson varieties for alternating heaps. Using work of Francone-Leclerc, we prove a conjecture of Hernandez-Zhang by identifying the above Grothendieck ring with a cluster algebra defined by Geiss-Hernandez-Leclerc. Our methods also yield an action of the Langlands dual group $G^{\vee}$ on this Grothendieck ring, and show that the shifted coproducts defined in work of the first and fifth authors with collaborators give rise to coproducts for truncated shifted Yangians. This machinery then allows us to prove further conjectures of Frenkel-Hernandez and Geiss-Hernandez-Leclerc on extended $QQ$-systems, and to obtain a generalization of a duality defined by Hernandez-Leclerc.
