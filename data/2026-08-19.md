### [1] [On detection probabilities of link invariants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05574)
*Tuomas Kelomäki,Abel Lacabanne,Daniel Tubbenhauer,Pedro Vaz,Victor L. Zhang*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文证明了许多标准链环不变量（如Jones多项式、HOMFLYPT多项式和积分Khovanov同调）在至多n个交叉的素交错链环中，不同不变量值的比例和检测概率都随n呈指数衰减，且具有显式的通用速率。几乎所有这样的链环都属于一个大小本身随n指数增长的不变量纤维。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究链环不变量在有限类链环中的区分能力和分布情况，理解不变量纤维的大小随交叉数增长的行为。

Method: 对标准链环不变量（Jones和HOMFLYPT多项式、积分Khovanov同调）在至多n个交叉的素交错链环上进行系统性分析，证明不变量值比例和检测概率的指数衰减，并计算显式的通用衰减速率。配套网站提供更详细的分布数据和拓扑数据分析。

Result: 对于多个标准链环不变量，在不同不变量值比例和检测概率两个方面都实现了指数衰减，且速率是显式且通用的。几乎所有链环都属于指数大小的不变量纤维。

Conclusion: 不变量纤维的指数增长是一个普遍现象，适用于Jones多项式、HOMFLYPT多项式和积分Khovanov同调等多种不变量，表明这些不变量在交错链环中的区分能力随交叉数增加而系统性减弱。

Abstract: We prove that, for many standard link invariants, both the proportion of distinct invariant values and the detection probability among prime alternating links with at most n crossings decay exponentially in n, with an explicit universal rate. In fact, almost every such link belongs to an invariant fiber whose size is itself exponential in n. This phenomenon applies broadly, in particular to the Jones and HOMFLYPT polynomials and integral Khovanov homology. The companion website gives a much more detailed view of the data, including complete distributions of fiber sizes, separate alternating and non-alternating data, and topological data analysis.

</details>

### [2] [Symmetry-enriched topological order in tensor networks: Defects, gauging and anyon condensation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.07982)
*Dominic J. Williamson,Nick Bultinck,Frank Verstraete*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究二维张量网络态中的对称性富集拓扑序，利用分级矩阵乘积算子代数表示对称性诱导的畴壁，建立了与分级酉融合范畴理论的紧密联系，并构造了所有畴壁的拓扑缺陷超选择扇区的张量网络表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对称性富集拓扑序是凝聚态物理中的重要研究方向，理解对称性如何作用于任意子以及对称性诱导的畴壁结构对于刻画拓扑序的本质至关重要。作者希望从张量网络的角度建立系统性的理论框架。

Method: 使用分级矩阵乘积算子（graded MPO）代数来表示对称性诱导的畴壁，建立了与分级酉融合范畴（graded unitary fusion categories）的对应关系，并据此构造拓扑缺陷超选择扇区的张量网络表示。

Result: 成功从张量网络表示中提取了涌现的对称性富集拓扑序，包括对称性对底层任意子的作用。分析了由规范全局对称性和玻色子子理论凝聚所诱导的对偶相变，推导出相变两侧拓扑序之间的关系，并给出了多个具体示例。

Conclusion: 该工作建立了一个统一的张量网络框架来理解和计算对称性富集拓扑序及其相变，为后续研究提供了理论工具和具体实例支撑。

Abstract: We study symmetry-enriched topological order in two-dimensional tensor network states by using graded matrix product operator algebras to represent symmetry-induced domain walls. A close connection to the theory of graded unitary fusion categories is established. Tensor network representations of the topological defect superselection sectors are constructed for all domain walls. The emergent symmetry-enriched topological order is extracted from these representations, including the symmetry action on the underlying anyons. Dual phase transitions, induced by gauging a global symmetry, and condensation of a bosonic subtheory, are analyzed and the relationship between topological orders on either side of the transition is derived. Several examples are worked through explicitly.

</details>

### [3] [Bilinear tau forms of quantum Painlevé equations and $\mathbb{C}^2/\mathbb{Z}_2$ blowup relations in SUSY gauge theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.25051)
*Giulio Bonelli,Anton Shchechkin,Alessandro Tanzini*

Main category: math-ph

TL;DR: 该论文推导了量子化的Painlevé方程的双线性tau形式，将其与从C²/Z₂ blowup关系得到的N=2超对称规范理论配分函数联系起来，并完全确定了精细Painlevé/规范理论词典。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者旨在建立量子Painlevé方程与超对称规范理论之间更深层的联系，完全固定精细化的对应词典，并探索量子Painlevé对称性在tau函数层面的表现。

Method: 作者推导了量子非自治Painlevé Hamiltonians的规范方程，从规范的量子Painlevé方程出发得到双线性tau形式，并在tau函数层面描述量子Painlevé对称性，作为副产品获得包含非平凡完整（holonomy）扇区的C²/Z₂ blowup关系。

Result: 成功将量子Painlevé方程与之前从C²/Z₂ blowup关系得到的规范理论配分函数相关联，完全确定了精细Painlevé/规范理论词典，并在tau函数层面描述了量子Painlevé对称性，同时获得了包含非平凡完整扇区的多个blowup关系。

Conclusion: 该工作深化了量子Painlevé方程与N=2超对称规范理论之间的联系，为Painlevé/规范理论对应提供了更完整的框架，并揭示了量子可积性在规范理论中的新体现。

Abstract: We derive bilinear tau forms of the canonically quantized Painlevé equations, thereby relating them to those previously obtained from the $\mathbb{C}^2/\mathbb{Z}_2$ blowup relations for the $\mathcal{N}=2$ supersymmetric gauge theory partition functions on a general $Ω$-background. We fully fix the refined Painlevé/gauge theory dictionary by formulating the proper equations for the quantum nonautonomous Painlevé Hamiltonians. We also describe the quantum Painlevé symmetries at the level of tau functions and, as a byproduct of this analysis, obtain several $\mathbb{C}^2/\mathbb{Z}_2$ blowup relations including those in nontrivial holonomy sectors of the gauge theory.

</details>

### [4] [Central extensions of mapping class groups of surfaces from stated skein algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24378)
*Joris Moulai*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文计算了与stated skein代数及有限维可分解ribbon Hopf代数相关的映射类群中心扩张，证明过程纯二维，不依赖TQFT论证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 映射类群的投影表示在低维拓扑和量子不变量研究中具有重要地位，而理解其中心扩张对于揭示表示的线性化结构十分关键。现有方法多依赖TQFT，本文希望提供一种纯二维的替代证明路径。

Method: 利用带亏格g、零个或一个边界分量及n个标记点的曲面Σ上的stated skein代数，结合有限维可分解ribbon Hopf代数H构造投影表示，并通过线性化该表示来计算映射类群的中心扩张，全程采用纯二维方法。

Result: 成功计算了与stated skein代数和H线性化投影表示相关联的映射类群中心扩张，且证明不借助任何TQFT论证。

Conclusion: 该结果提供了一个自足的二维框架来理解映射类群的中心扩张，为skein理论与映射类群表示之间的联系提供了新的视角和证明途径。

Abstract: Let $Σ$ be a surface of genus $g$ with zero or one boundary component and $n$ marked points, and $H$ a finite-dimensional factorizable ribbon Hopf algebra. We compute the central extension of the mapping class group of $Σ$, associated with a linearization of the projective representation constructed from the stated skein algebra of $Σ$ and $H$. Our proof is purely two-dimensional, and makes no use of TQFT arguments.

</details>
