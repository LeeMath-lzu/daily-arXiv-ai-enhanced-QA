### [1] [$C_2$-Cofiniteness and Rationality of the Icosahedral Orbifold $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13872)
*Shun Xu*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文研究了秩一根格顶点算子代数 $V_{L_2}$ 在二十面体群 $A_5$ 作用下的不动点子代数 $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$，证明了它是 $C_2$-有限且强有理的。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究有限群作用下格顶点算子代数不动点子代数的理性性质，特别是通过二十面体群 $A_5$（作为 $PSL_2(\mathbb{C})$ 的有限子群）作用来构造并验证新的强有理顶点算子代数。

Method: 利用 $A_5$ 通过二十面体子群嵌入 $\operatorname{Aut}(V_{L_2})\cong PSL_2(\mathbb{C})$ 作用在 $V_{L_2}$ 上，考察其不动点子代数 $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$，并通过代数和表示论方法验证其 $C_2$-有限性和强理性。

Result: 证明了不动点顶点算子代数 $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$ 满足 $C_2$-有限性条件，并且是强有理的。

Conclusion: 该结果确认了 $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$ 是一个强有理顶点算子代数，丰富了有限群作用构造强有理顶点算子代数的例子类别。

Abstract: Let $L_2=\mathbb{Z}α$ be the rank-one root lattice with $(α,α)=2$, and let $A_5$ act on the lattice vertex operator algebra $V_{L_2}$ through an icosahedral subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(V_{L_2})\cong PSL_2(\mathbb{C})$. We prove that the fixed-point vertex operator algebra $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$ is $C_2$-cofinite and strongly rational.

### [2] [Miura operators as R-matrices from M-brane intersections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15990)
*Nathan Haouzi,Saebyeok Jeong*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 该论文提出Miura算子是某类无限维量子代数的R-矩阵，并通过量子环面代数实现q-形变的W-和Y-代数的Miura算子来验证这一猜想，同时在M理论和超对称规范理论的物理框架中给出了具体构造。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 希望通过将Miura算子与R-矩阵建立联系，统一量子代数中两类重要的代数结构，并借助物理框架（M理论、Chern-Simons理论、超对称规范理论）为这一数学联系提供物理解释和应用。

Method: 首先在量子环面代数中构造Miura算子的R-矩阵实现；然后利用5维非交换Chern-Simons理论中拓扑线缺陷与全纯面缺陷的规范不变交点来描述M2-M5膜交叉，从而将每个R-矩阵与Miura算子对应；通过Type IIB对偶将Miura变换的分量与3维超对称规范理论的半指标联系起来；最后从Miura变换代数地构造qq-特征标。

Result: 成功建立了Miura算子与R-矩阵之间的一一对应关系；在M理论的Ω-背景中通过M2-M5膜交叉实现了Miura变换的工程化构造；在对偶的Type IIB框架中，Miura变换分量被证明与D3-NS5 Hanany-Witten系统的3维规范理论半指标一致；并展示了qq-特征标可从Miura变换中代数地构造出来。

Conclusion: 该工作为Miura算子和R-矩阵建立了深刻联系，将量子代数、可积系统、M理论和超对称规范理论统一在同一个框架中，并为qq-特征标的构造提供了新的代数途径。

Abstract: We propose that Miura operators are R-matrices of certain infinite-dimensional quantum algebras. We test our proposal by realizing Miura operators of $q$-deformed $W$- and $Y$-algebras in terms of R-matrices of the quantum toroidal algebra of $\mathfrak{gl}(1)$. Physically, the representations of this toroidal algebra arise from the algebra of local operators on M2-branes and M5-branes, in M-theory subject to an $Ω$-background. We associate an R-matrix to each M2-M5 brane crossing, by studying its description as a gauge-invariant intersection of a topological line defect and a holomorphic surface defect in 5-dimensional non-commutative Chern-Simons theory. The Miura transformation is engineered using multiple M2-M5 intersections, relying crucially on the properties of the underlying R-matrices. We thereby identify each R-matrix with a Miura operator. In a dual Type IIB frame, the components of the Miura transformation are shown to coincide with the half-index of a 3d supersymmetric gauge theory on a Hanany-Witten system of D3-NS5 branes. As a further application, we demonstrate that $qq$-characters can be algebraically constructed from the Miura transformation.

</details>
