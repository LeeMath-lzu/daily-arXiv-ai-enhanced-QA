### [1] [Decomposition matrices of cyclotomic $q$-web categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07394)
*Linliang Song,Weiqiang Wang*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究了分圆q-网的末端代数（endomorphism algebras）的胞腔结构。这是一个介于分圆Hecke代数和分圆q-Schur代数之间的新量子代数族。作者证明了在q为一般参数或单位根时，分圆q-网的模范畴的Grothendieck群同构于量子仿射gl_p上的可积最高权模，且该同构将投射不可分解模的类映射到典范基。这大幅推广了Lascoux-Leclerc-Thibon、Ariki和Varagnolo-Vasserot的经典工作。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典工作（LLT猜想、Ariki定理、Varagnolo-Vasserot定理）建立了Hecke代数和q-Schur代数与量子仿射李代数表示之间的联系，但分圆q-网作为介于两者之间的新代数族，其胞腔理论及Grothendieck群与量子群表示的联系尚未被建立，本文旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 作者为分圆q-网的末端代数构造了胞腔结构（cellular structures），并利用这一结构研究其模范畴的Grothendieck群。通过建立Grothendieck群与量子仿射gl_p的不可约最高权模之间的同构，并证明该同构将投射不可分解模对应到典范基。

Result: 证明了分圆q-网的Grothendieck群在q为一般参数或单位根时均同构于量子仿射gl_p的不可约最高权模，且投射不可分解模的类一一对应于典范基元素。该结果统一并推广了LLT、Ariki和Varagnolo-Vasserot的经典结论。

Conclusion: 分圆q-网代数提供了连接分圆Hecke代数和分圆q-Schur代数的统一框架，其表示论与量子仿射李代数理论之间的深刻联系得到了系统性的建立与推广。

Abstract: We develop the cellular structures for the endomorphism algebras of cyclotomic $q$-webs, which form a new family of quantum algebras sitting in between cyclotomic Hecke algebras and cyclotomic $q$-Schur algebras. We show that the Grothendieck group of a module category of the cyclotomic $q$-webs for $q$ generic or a root of unity is isomorphic to an integrable highest weight module over quantum affine $\mathfrak{gl}_p$; moreover, the isomorphism maps the classes of projective indecomposable modules to the canonical basis. This substantially generalizes the classic works of Lascoux-Leclerc-Thibon, Ariki, and Varagnolo-Vasserot.

### [2] [Comparing self-dual corings, Frobenius corings and Ringel self-duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03299)
*Tomasz Brzeziński,Teresa Conde,Steffen Koenig,Julian Külshammer*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文对自对偶余环（self-dual corings）和自对偶代数扩张进行了分类，并将其与Ringel自对偶进行对比，揭示了二者之间的根本差异。研究表明，对每个余环C可关联左右两个对偶代数，二者自然一致当且仅当C是Frobenius余环；文中还从Frobenius扩张和Frobenius函子的角度给出了自对偶的多种等价刻画，并给出了Ringel自对偶不同的同调解释。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望系统分类自对偶余环与自对偶代数扩张，并厘清这类（自）对偶与Ringel（自）对偶之间的本质区别，填补已有文献中对这些对偶概念之间关系的理解空白。

Method: 通过将每个余环C关联左右两个对偶代数，并利用Frobenius余环、Frobenius扩张以及Frobenius函子等工具，给出C为自对偶的多种等价刻画，同时从同调视角解释Ringel自对偶的独特性质。

Result: 证明了左右对偶代数自然一致当且仅当C为Frobenius余环；给出了自对偶在Frobenius扩张和Frobenius函子框架下的多种等价刻画；并揭示Ringel自对偶与上述自对偶存在根本性差异，且给出了同调层面的解释。

Conclusion: 自对偶余环/代数扩张的对偶性与Ringel自对偶本质上不同，前者可由Frobenius结构完全刻画，后者则需要从同调的角度加以理解。

Abstract: Self-dual corings and self-dual algebra extensions are classified and these (self-)dualities are compared with Ringel (self-)duality, revealing fundamental differences. To each coring $\mathcal C$, two algebras are associated, known as the left and the right dual algebra of $\mathcal C$. It is shown that these two algebras coincide in a natural way if and only if $\mathcal C$ is a Frobenius coring. Various other equivalent characterisations of $\mathcal C$ being self-dual are given, in terms of certain algebra extensions being Frobenius extensions and in terms of certain forgetful or restriction functors being Frobenius functors. Ringel self-duality however is shown to be rather different, for which a homological explanation is given.

</details>

### [3] [Topological defects in reflection positive topological field theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07217)
*Lukas Müller*

Main category: math-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了反射正定拓扑缺陷量子场论中缺陷范畴的额外结构，定义了反射缺陷TQFT，并证明在二维情形下缺陷双范畴具有O(2)-dagger双范畴结构，若理论反射正定，还可装备与3-Hilbert空间相关的额外结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 拓扑缺陷被认为应组装成具有额外结构的高阶范畴，已有的精确化工作针对二维和三维定向拓扑量子场论。但反射正定性这一物理上重要的额外条件在这些范畴上会引入何种结构此前尚未被系统研究。

Method: 将反射缺陷TQFT定义为从缺陷流形范畴到对称幺半范畴的对称幺半函子，要求其将定向反转与复共轭交织；在此基础上引入正定性条件。在二维情形构造性地证明缺陷双范畴具有O(2)-dagger双范畴结构，并进一步建立与3-Hilbert空间定义的对应关系。

Result: 主要结果是二维反射缺陷TQFT的缺陷双范畴自然携带O(2)-dagger双范畴结构（该结构在文中被显式定义）；对于反射正定理论，该双范畴可装备与3-Hilbert空间定义高度一致的额外结构（在某些有限性和完备性条件下二者一致）。

Conclusion: 该工作将反射正定性这一关键物理性质系统地纳入缺陷高阶范畴的结构研究中，建立了O(2)-dagger双范畴与3-Hilbert空间之间的联系，为理解拓扑量子场论中缺陷的数学结构提供了新的框架。

Abstract: Topological defects in quantum field theories are believed to assemble into higher categories with extra structure. This has been made precise for defects in $2$- and $3$-dimensional oriented topological quantum field theories by Davydov, Kong, and Runkel and by Carqueville, Meusburger, and Schaumann, respectively. In this paper we study the extra structure present on these categories when the topological field theory is additionally reflection positive. We define reflection defect TQFTs as symmetric monoidal functors out of a defect bordism category that intertwine orientation reversal with complex conjugation; they are reflection positive if they satisfy an additional positivity condition. Our main result is that in two dimensions the bicategory of defects $\mathcal{T}_\mathcal{Z}$ associated to a reflection defect TQFT carries the natural structure of an $O(2)$-dagger bicategory, a structure we define explicitly. If the theory is reflection positive, $\mathcal{T}_\mathcal{Z}$ can be equipped with additional structure closely related to the definition of a 3-Hilbert space (the two agree up to some finiteness and completeness conditions).

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

### [6] [A Kohno--Drinfeld Theorem for iquantum Weyl groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06473)
*Elijah Bodish,Ivan Motorin*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文证明了辫群的两个有限维线性表示是同构的：一个来自边界Casimir联络的幺单性，另一个来自ι量子Weyl群。证明基于(O_m, so_{2n})自旋Howe对偶性，因此仅适用于so_m ⊂ sl_m这一具体对称对。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 该论文旨在建立辫群理论中两个看似不同来源的表示之间的同构关系，即边界Casimir联络的幺单性与ι量子Weyl群表示的对应。这一同构关系对于深入理解量子群、辫群表示以及相关的数学物理结构具有重要意义。作者通过具体的对称对（so_m ⊂ sl_m）来验证这一猜想性的联系。

Method: 论文的方法核心是利用(O_m, so_{2n})自旋Howe对偶性来建立两个表示之间的同构。具体而言，边界Casimir联络的幺单性表示和ι量子Weyl群表示都是在辫群上定义的，作者借助自旋Howe对偶的特殊结构，在so_m ⊂ sl_m的对称对情形下证明了这两个表示的等价性。

Result: 成功证明了辫群的这两个有限维线性表示是同构的，但证明仅对so_m ⊂ sl_m这一特定的对称对有效，适用于so_m的任意可积表示。

Abstract: We prove that two finite-dimensional linear representations of the braid group are isomorphic. ### [7] [Coefficients of $q$-real numbers: their combinatorial meaning and growth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06761)
*Pavel Etingof,Valentin Ovsienko*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究q-形变实数的系数，给出其组合解释，并证明q-形变黄金比在所有正实数对应的q-实数中具有最小的收敛半径，这是经典Hurwitz定理的q-类比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Morier-Genoud和本文第二作者定义了q-形变实数，其系数作为实数轴上的函数尚未被系统研究。此前多位研究者提出了一个猜想：q-形变黄金比具有最小的收敛半径。本文旨在证明该猜想并给出系数的组合解释。

Method: 采用组合方法。对每个实数x∈(1,2)，将q-实数[x]_q的系数与q-形变黄金比的对应系数进行比较。核心工具是引入与q-实数相关联的有序有根树的集合，并证明黄金比对应一类普适的树结构。

Result: 证明了对于区间(1,2)内的每个实数x，q-实数[x]_q的每个系数的绝对值均被q-形变黄金比对应系数的绝对值所控制。由此证明了q-形变黄金比具有最小收敛半径的猜想。

Conclusion: 通过组合方法（有序有根树）成功证明了q-形变黄金比收敛半径最小的猜想，实现了经典Hurwitz定理的q-类比，并为q-实数系数提供了组合解释。

Abstract: A $q$-deformed real number, or ``$q$-real'', was defined by Morier-Genoud and the second author. When $x\in\mathbb{R}$ such that $x\geq0$, the $q$-analogue $[x]_q$ is a power series with integer coefficients in one formal variable~$q$. In general a $q$-real is a formal Laurent series. The main goal of this paper is to study the coefficients of $q$-reals as functions on~$\mathbb{R}$ and give a combinatorial interpretation of these coefficients. This allows us to prove a conjecture studied by several authors stating that the $q$-deformed golden ratio has the smallest radius of convergence among the radii of the $q$-reals associated with positive real numbers. This is a $q$-analogue of the classical Hurwitz theorem. Our approach is combinatorial. We prove that for every real number $x$ in the interval $(1,2)$ the absolute value of each coefficient of the power series representing the $q$-real $[x]_q$ is dominated by the absolute value of the corresponding coefficient of the $q$-deformed golden ratio. The main notion is a certain collection of ordered rooted trees associated with a $q$-real. We prove that the golden ratio corresponds to a universal class of trees.

</details>
