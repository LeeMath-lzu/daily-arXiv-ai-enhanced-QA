### [1] [Transparent Subalgebras and Local Module Categories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19153)
*Kenichi Shimizu,Harshit Yadav*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文研究了辫子有限张量范畴中交换简单代数的最大透明子代数，将其识别为自由模函子的中心提升所诱导的代数，并给出了局部模范畴的Frobenius-Perron维数和Müger中心的公式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解交换代数在辫子有限张量范畴中的透明子代数结构，以及局部模范畴的非退化性、对称性和模性，是张量范畴理论中的核心问题。作者希望给出系统性的刻画和定量公式。

Method: 通过将最大透明子代数识别为自由模函子中心提升诱导的代数，利用该识别推导Frobenius-Perron维数和Müger中心的显式公式，并证明相对中心分解定理及相对Witt类的保持性。

Result: 得到了局部A-模范畴的非退化性、对称性和模性的判据，以及FPdim的锐利上界；将辫子范畴的Müger中心实现为伴随代数上的局部模范畴；证明了取局部模范畴保持相对Witt类。

Conclusion: 本文建立了交换代数的最大透明子代数与自由模函子中心提升之间的深刻联系，提供了局部模范畴的完整结构理论，并给出了相对Witt类的重要结论。

Abstract: Let $A$ be a commutative simple algebra in a braided finite tensor category $\mathcal{B}$. We identify the largest transparent subalgebra of $A$ as the algebra induced by a central lift of the free-module functor. This identification gives formulas for the Frobenius-Perron dimension and the Müger center of the category of local $A$-modules. These formulas give criteria for nondegeneracy, symmetry, and modularity, together with sharp bounds on $\mathrm{FPdim}_{\mathcal{B}}(A)$. We also realize the Müger center of $\mathcal{B}$ as a category of local modules over an adjoint algebra. Finally, we prove a relative-center factorization and deduce that taking the category of local modules preserves the relative Witt class.

### [2] [Goldman-Turaev formality from the Kontsevich integral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20983)
*Dror Bar-Natan,Zsuzsanna Dancso,Tamara Hogan,Jessica Liu,Nancy Scherich*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种解决带框架的Goldman-Turaev李双代数形式化问题的新方法，利用Kontsevich积分构造Goldman-Turaev同态展开（形式化同构），并通过对厚穿孔圆盘中缠绕的低度Vassiliev商（"涌现"商）进行三维推导来完成证明。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的Massuyeau（2018）证明依赖于辫群方法，作者希望提供一种不同的证明途径，同时解决技术上具有挑战性的Turaev余括号的相容性问题。

Method: 从厚穿孔圆盘中的缠绕出发，通过求模Conway skein关系得到低度Vassiliev商——"涌现"商，由此三维地推导出Goldman-Turaev李双代数，并基于Kontsevich积分构造同态展开。

Result: 成功构造了Goldman-Turaev同态展开（形式化同构），并证明了其与Goldman括号和Turaev余括号均相容，提供了一个通用的概念框架来解决形式化问题。

Conclusion: 该工作为Goldman-Turaev李双代数的形式化问题提供了独立于辫方法的新的三维证明途径，并建立了通用的相容性证明框架。

Abstract: We present a new solution to the formality problem for the framed Goldman--Turaev Lie bialgebra, constructing Goldman-Turaev homomorphic expansions (formality isomorphisms) from the Kontsevich integral. Our proof uses a three dimensional derivation of the Goldman-Turaev Lie biaglebra arising from a low-degree Vassiliev quotient -- the {\em emergent} quotient -- of tangles in a thickened punctured disk, modulo a Conway skein relation. This is in contrast to Massuyeau's 2018 proof using braids. A feature of our approach is a general conceptual framework which is applied to prove the compatibility of the homomorphic expansion with both the Goldman bracket and the technically challenging Turaev cobracket.

</details>


### [3] [Non-semisimple Crane-Yetter theory varying over the character stack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19667)
*Patrick Kinnear*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文构建了一个四维Crane-Yetter拓扑量子场论的非半单相对版本，该理论相对于五维经典G-规范理论定义，并证明了其相对可逆性，推广了原始Crane-Yetter理论的关键性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 原始Crane-Yetter理论的可逆性使其能够捕捉Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev理论的framing反常，作者希望将该可逆性推广到非半单数据以及相对（五维）框架下，从而为量子场论和低维拓扑提供更一般的工具。

Method: 构建了一个相对于五维经典G-规范理论的四维Crane-Yetter TQFT相对版本，该理论利用非半单数据，并通过对流形赋予特征栈（character stack）的相应线性化来实现。关键是通过gauging过程将构造视为经典规范理论的拓扑对称性缺陷，并证明其可逆性。

Result: 建立了构造的相对可逆性：在曲面层面，该可逆性暗示了skein代数的范畴化、stack化的唯一性定理；在三维流形层面，它为特征栈配备了一个典范线丛。

Conclusion: 本文通过gauging过程证明了该拓扑对称性缺陷的可逆性，推广了原始Crane-Yetter理论的可逆性性质，为非半单量子不变量和经典规范理论之间的联系提供了新框架。

Abstract: We construct a relative version of the Crane-Yetter topological quantum field theory in four dimensions, from non-semisimple data. Our theory is defined relative to the classical $G$-gauge theory in five dimensions -- this latter theory assigns to each manifold $M$ the appropriate linearization of the moduli stack of $G$-local systems, called the character stack. Our main result is to establish a relative invertibility property for our construction. This invertibility generalizes the key invertibility property of the original Crane-Yetter theory which allowed it to capture the framing anomaly of the celebrated Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev theory. In particular our invertibilty statement at the level of surfaces implies a categorical, stacky version of the unicity theorem for skein algebras; at the level of 3-manifolds it equips the character stack with a canonical line bundle. Regarded as a topological symmetry defect of classical gauge theory, our work establishes invertibility of this defect by a gauging procedure.

### [4] [On the computation of the canonical basis for irreducible highest weight $U_q (\mathfrak{gl}_{\infty})$-module](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16889)
*Nicolas Jacon,Abel Lacabanne*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究量子群$U_q(\mathfrak{gl}_\infty)$高级Fock空间中的典范基元素，将Leclerc-Miyachi公式推广到任意阶，通过引入基于符号的新显式构造（包括列移除定理和若干闭式公式），为典范基元素提供了显式描述，并应用于Calogero-Moser胞腔特征标和Ariki-Koike代数的分解矩阵。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高级Fock空间中的典范基元素与复反射群的Calogero-Moser理论存在猜想性联系，但此前缺乏对任意阶情形的显式描述方法，Leclerc-Miyachi公式仅适用于特定情形，需要更一般的构造工具。

Method: 基于符号（symbols）引入新的显式构造，推广Leclerc-Miyachi公式至任意阶；建立列移除定理（column removal theorem）；在多种情形下推导出闭式公式，给出典范基元素的显式描述。

Result: 成功将Leclerc-Miyachi公式推广到任意阶，获得了典范基元素的显式描述，并应用于Calogero-Moser胞腔特征标以及Ariki-Koike代数的分解矩阵的计算。

Conclusion: 本文提供的显式构造为高级Fock空间中典范基元素的研究提供了新工具，同时通过其应用验证了与Calogero-Moser理论和Ariki-Koike代数分解矩阵之间的联系，推动了相关猜想的发展。

Abstract: We study canonical basis elements in higher-level Fock spaces associated with the quantum group $U_q(\mathfrak{gl}_\infty)$, which are conjecturally related to Calogero-Moser theory for complex reflection groups. We generalize the Leclerc-Miyachi formula to arbitrary levels by introducing new explicit constructions based on symbols, including a column removal theorem and closed formulas in several cases. These results provide explicit descriptions of canonical basis elements with applications to Calogero-Moser cellular characters and to the decomposition matrices of Ariki-Koike algebras.

</details>
